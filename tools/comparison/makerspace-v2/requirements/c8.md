# Makerspace change C8: 15-minute booking granularity and new opening hours

Applies to the requirements after C7. Section numbers refer to [w0.md](w0.md), including its section 16 "Architecture".

## What changes

The workshop extends its opening hours, opens on Sunday afternoons and lets members book in 15-minute steps.

- **Booking granularity (section 4.4) is 15 minutes.** A reservation's start and end must each fall on a minute that is a multiple of 15 within the hour (`:00`, `:15`, `:30` or `:45`). A reservation lasts at least one granularity step, now 15 minutes, and at most 240 minutes, as before.
- **`createReservation`:** `invalid_slot` now applies when `start` or `end` is not on a 15-minute boundary or the length is outside 15 to 240 minutes.
- **Opening hours (section 4.4)** are now:

  | Day | Opens | Closes | Open minutes |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | Monday to Thursday | 08:00 | 22:00 | 840 |
  | Friday | 08:00 | 20:00 | 720 |
  | Saturday | 09:00 | 17:00 | 480 |
  | Sunday | 12:00 | 16:00 | 240 |

  Every day now has an opening period, so `getMachineSchedule` always returns an `openingHours` object and never `null`.
- **Overview (section 1):** members reserve machines in 15-minute steps.
- The new hours and granularity apply to everything that uses them: the reservation rules, `getMachineSchedule` and the `openMinutes` of the utilization report, for every date, including dates before the change.

The examples below replace the example after `createReservation` in section 9.1, which no longer holds (Saturday now closes at 17:00, and `10:15` is now a valid boundary).

Examples: on Sunday `2026-03-08`, `12:00`–`12:15` is accepted and `11:45`–`12:15` is refused with `outside_opening_hours`. On Friday `2026-03-06`, `19:45`–`20:00` is inside opening hours and `19:45`–`20:15` is not. On Tuesday `2026-03-03`, `10:15`–`10:30` is accepted and `10:10`–`10:30` is refused with `invalid_slot`.

## What stays as it is

- Maintenance windows and lockouts may still use any minute and are not limited to opening hours.
- Check-in and check-out times are not tied to the granularity; metering still counts every minute.
- The maximum reservation length of 240 minutes, the half-open interval rule, the order of refusals and every other rule are unchanged.
