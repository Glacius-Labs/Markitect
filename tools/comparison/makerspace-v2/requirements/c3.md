# Makerspace change C3: bulk price tiers for materials

Applies to the requirements after C2. Section numbers refer to [w0.md](w0.md), including its section 16 "Architecture".

## What changes

Material purchases get a bulk discount that depends on the quantity of the purchase. Until now every purchase has `discountPercent` 0.

| Quantity of the purchase | `discountPercent` |
| --- | --- |
| 1 to 9 | 0 |
| 10 to 24 | 5 |
| 25 or more | 12 |

- The tier is chosen by the `quantity` of the single purchase. Quantities of separate purchases, also by the same member or of the same material, are never added together.
- `purchaseMaterial` (section 10) sets the purchase's `discountPercent` from this table instead of 0. The purchase's `amountCents` and the material charge are calculated with the formula of section 4.3, rounded half up once per line, as before. The material charge carries the same `discountPercent` and `amountCents` as the purchase.
- The tier table is the same for every material and every member. The member's membership tier plays no part in it.

Examples, with the formula `(quantity × unitPriceCents × (100 − discountPercent) + 50) div 100`:

| Purchase | Calculation | `amountCents` |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 9 units at 199 | `(9 × 199 × 100 + 50) div 100` | 1791 |
| 10 units at 199 | `(10 × 199 × 95 + 50) div 100` | 1891 |
| 25 units at 37 | `(25 × 37 × 88 + 50) div 100` | 814 |

## What must not change

The bulk discount is a materials rule only. Nothing outside material purchases changes:

- **Bookings:** reservations, their rules, check-in, check-out, metering and usage charges, including the usage discount by membership tier, stay exactly as they are.
- **Memberships:** tiers, monthly fees, the usage discount table, standing and the membership line stay exactly as they are. The tier usage discount never applies to materials, and the bulk discount never applies to usage.
- **Training:** courses, course fees, enrollments, course charges and certifications stay exactly as they are.
- **Everything else:** unit prices, stock rules, the order of refusals in `purchaseMaterial`, invoice generation, payments, credit, the reports' definitions (the revenue report's `materialCents` is still the sum of the material line amounts), audit, authorization, time rules, records and refusal codes. No operation, argument, field or code is added or removed.
