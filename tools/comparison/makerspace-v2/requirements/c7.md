# Makerspace change C7: payments folded into invoices

Applies to the requirements after C6. Section numbers refer to [w0.md](w0.md), including its section 16 "Architecture".

## What changes

Only the way billing is described. Until now billing has two parts, "Charges and invoices" (section 11.1) and "Payments and credit" (section 11.2). From this change on, billing is one area, invoices, and payments and credit are described as part of an invoice's life. The replacement text for the whole of section 11 follows below.

There is **no behavior change**. No operation, argument, result field, record, refusal code, order of checks, ordering of lists, amount, rounding rule, time rule, permission, audit entry or operation name changes. The operation index keeps the area "Billing" for the same five operations. A program that meets the requirements after C6 meets the requirements after C7 without any change, and any difference in observable behavior is a defect.

## What must not change

Every billing behavior as it stands after C6, and every other section. That includes the lockout credits that C2 added to the credit balance (sections 4.3 and 13) and the overdue block and the account's `blocked` field that C5 added. The text below restates that behavior; where it seems to differ from the earlier text, the earlier behavior is meant.

## Replacement text for section 11

> ## 11 Billing: invoices
>
> Billing is organized around the invoice. An invoice collects one member's charges for one calendar month. It is due 14 days after it is issued, receives payments until it is settled, and passes any overpayment to the member's credit, which in turn settles part of the member's later invoices.
>
> **Charges.** Usage, course and material charges collect on the member's account until they are invoiced. A charge belongs to the month of its `at`. A charge is unbilled until it appears on an invoice.
>
> **Issuing invoices.** Invoices are generated per calendar month, for all members at once, when staff request it. Generation for a month is possible once the month has ended at the request time, and only once per month. Months can be generated in any order. For a month M, every member whose `registeredAt` is before the first minute of the month after M receives one invoice, whatever the member's standing. The invoice is built as follows:
>
> 1. `invoiceId` is the next invoice identifier. Members are processed in ascending `memberId` order.
> 2. `issuedAt` is the request time; `dueAt` is 14 days after `issuedAt`.
> 3. `lines` starts with the membership line: `kind` `membership`, `ref` the member's current tier, `quantity` 1, `unitPriceCents` the tier's monthly fee, `discountPercent` 0, `at` the request time. All of the member's unbilled charges whose `at` lies in month M follow, ordered as section 4.5 states. These charges are no longer unbilled.
> 4. `totalCents` is the sum of the line amounts.
> 5. `creditAppliedCents` is the smaller of the member's credit balance and `totalCents`. The credit balance decreases by that amount.
> 6. `paidCents` is 0. `balanceCents` is `totalCents − creditAppliedCents − paidCents`.
> 7. `status` is `paid` when `balanceCents` is 0, otherwise `open`.
>
> Because request times never go backwards and generation needs the month to have ended, no charge can be created for a month after that month was invoiced.
>
> **Due date, overdue and block.** An invoice is overdue at a request time when its status is `open` and the request time is at or after `dueAt`. The `overdue` field reports this at the request time of the response. A member is blocked at a request time when at least one of the member's invoices has status `open` and the request time is more than 30 days after its `dueAt`, that is, later than `dueAt` plus 43200 minutes. A blocked member cannot make reservations (`account_overdue`, section 9.1) or buy materials (`account_overdue`, section 10), whoever acts: the member, staff or an admin. The block ends only when each such invoice reaches status `paid`. Apart from the block, being overdue has no effect.
>
> **Paying an invoice.** A payment pays one invoice. The part of it that exceeds the invoice's balance becomes credit on the member's account. Paying after the due date is allowed; an invoice that is `paid` takes no further payment.
>
> **Credit.** Each member has a credit balance, initially 0. It increases by overpayments, by credits an admin issues as a goodwill gesture and by lockout credits (section 12). Credit is applied automatically to invoices generated later, as step 5 above describes; it is never applied to an invoice that already exists and is never paid out.
>
> Worked example: member `ada` on tier `plus` uses a machine with rate 45 from `2026-03-03T10:00Z` to `10:37`. At check-out a usage charge of 1499 cents is created (section 4.3). Generating `2026-03` at `2026-04-01T08:00Z` gives `ada` an invoice with the membership line (4900) and the usage line (1499): `totalCents` 6399, due `2026-04-15T08:00Z`.
>
> #### `generateInvoices`
>
> Mutation.
>
> - Arguments: `month` (month).
> - Refusals, in this order: `month_not_ended` when the request time is before the first minute of the following month (for `2026-03`, before `2026-04-01T00:00Z`); `already_invoiced` when invoices for this month were generated before.
> - Effect: creates the invoices as described above and marks the month as invoiced, even when there is no member to invoice.
> - Result: `month` and `invoices`, the invoices created by this request.
>
> #### `listInvoices`
>
> Read. Arguments: `memberId` (identifier, an existing member). Result: `memberId` and `invoices`, all of the member's invoices.
>
> #### `recordPayment`
>
> Mutation.
>
> - Arguments: `invoiceId` (identifier, an existing invoice), `amountCents` (integer 1 to 100000000).
> - Refusals: `invoice_paid` when the invoice's status is `paid`.
> - Effect: `appliedCents` is the smaller of `amountCents` and the invoice's `balanceCents`; `creditedCents` is `amountCents − appliedCents`. The invoice's `paidCents` increases and its `balanceCents` decreases by `appliedCents`; when `balanceCents` reaches 0 its status becomes `paid`. The member's credit balance increases by `creditedCents`. A payment record is created with `memberId` the invoice's member (whoever acts) and `at` the request time.
> - Result: `payment`, `invoice` (after the change) and `creditBalanceCents`, the member's credit balance after the change.
>
> #### `issueCredit`
>
> Mutation.
>
> - Arguments: `memberId` (identifier, an existing member), `amountCents` (integer 1 to 100000000), `reason` (text).
> - Effect: the member's credit balance increases by `amountCents`. The credit counts as issued at the request time (section 13).
> - Result: `memberId`, `amountCents` and `creditBalanceCents`, the member's credit balance after the change.
>
> #### `getAccount`
>
> Read. Arguments: `memberId` (identifier, an existing member). Result: `account`, where:
>
> - `creditBalanceCents` is the member's credit balance;
> - `openBalanceCents` is the sum of `balanceCents` over the member's invoices with status `open`;
> - `overdueBalanceCents` is the same sum over the member's overdue invoices;
> - `unbilledCents` is the sum of the amounts of the member's unbilled charges;
> - `blocked` states whether the member is blocked at the request time.

References elsewhere to "section 11.1" or "section 11.2" now refer to section 11.
