# Makerspace change C5: long-overdue invoices block reservations and purchases

Applies to the requirements after C4. Section numbers refer to [w0.md](w0.md).

## What changes

Until now an overdue invoice has no effect beyond its `overdue` flag. From this change on, an invoice that is more than 30 days overdue blocks the member from making new reservations and buying materials until it is paid.

- **Blocked member (new, section 11.1):** a member is blocked at a request time when at least one of the member's invoices has status `open` and the request time is more than 30 days after its `dueAt`, that is, later than `dueAt` plus 43200 minutes. At exactly `dueAt` plus 30 days the member is not yet blocked; one minute later the member is blocked.
- **Unblocking:** the block ends as soon as no invoice meets that condition, which happens only when each such invoice reaches status `paid`. A partial payment that leaves a balance does not end the block. Credit is never applied to an existing invoice, so issuing credit does not end it either.
- **`createReservation` (section 9.1):** a new reservation rule follows rule 3: the member is not blocked. The new refusal `account_overdue` is checked after `member_suspended` and before `not_certified`.
- **`purchaseMaterial` (section 10):** the new refusal `account_overdue` is checked after `member_suspended` and before `insufficient_stock`.
- The block applies whoever acts: a member for themselves, staff or an admin.
- **`getAccount` (section 11.2):** the account record gets a new field `blocked`, a boolean that states whether the member is blocked at the request time. The account record (section 5) is now: `memberId`, `creditBalanceCents`, `openBalanceCents`, `overdueBalanceCents`, `unbilledCents`, `blocked`.
- **Refusal codes (section 3.2):** new code `account_overdue`: the member has an invoice that is more than 30 days overdue.
- **Overdue effect (section 11.1):** the sentence "Being overdue has no other effect." now reads "Apart from the block, being overdue has no effect."
- **Time-dependent states (section 4.4):** the list also names a blocked member.
- **Computed values (section 2.8):** the block compares the request time with `dueAt` plus 43200 minutes, a point that can lie as late as `2100-02-13T23:59Z`. The end-of-calendar list gains a consequence: an invoice whose `dueAt` is `2099-12-01T23:59Z` or later can never block, because no request time is later than `2099-12-31T23:59Z`.
- **Overview (section 1):** the first cross-area rule also requires that the member is not blocked; the Billing bullet says that an invoice more than 30 days overdue blocks reservations and purchases until it is paid.

Example: an invoice issued at `2026-04-01T08:00Z` is due at `2026-04-15T08:00Z`. While it is open, a reservation request at `2026-05-15T08:00Z` is not blocked; one at `2026-05-15T08:01Z` is refused with `account_overdue`.

## What stays as it is

- The block is not a standing. A blocked member's standing stays `good` unless staff suspend the member; `getMember` and `setMemberStanding` are unchanged.
- Existing reservations stay. Check-in, check-out, cancellation, course enrollment, payments, credits and invoice generation are not blocked.
- The `overdue` flag and `overdueBalanceCents` keep their meaning: an invoice is overdue from `dueAt` on, but it blocks only after more than 30 days.
- All other operations, codes, orders and permissions are unchanged.
