# Makerspace change C6: certifications no longer expire

Applies to the requirements after C5. Section numbers refer to [w0.md](w0.md).

## What changes

Certifications no longer have an expiry. A certification stays valid from its issue until it is revoked. Everything that existed only because of expiry is removed.

- **Validity (section 7.2):** a certification is valid on a date D when it is not revoked and `issuedOn ≤ D`. Reservations and check-ins always lie on or after the issue date, so in practice the reservation rule and the check-in rule now only require a certification for the machine's family that is not revoked.
- **Certification record (section 5):** the fields are now `memberId`, `familyId`, `issuedOn` and `status` (`valid` or `revoked`). The field `expiresOn` is removed and must not appear in any result. The status `expired` no longer exists.
- **Issue and reissue:** completing a course still issues the certification with `issuedOn` the date of the request time, and still replaces an existing certification for that family, revoked or not, with a new valid one.
- **`revokeCertification`:** refused with `not_certified` when the member has no certification for the family or it is already revoked, as before; the sentence about expired certifications no longer applies.
- **`not_certified` (section 3.2)** for reservations and check-ins now means that the member has no certification for the machine's family that is not revoked.
- **Time-dependent states (section 4.4):** the expired certification is removed from the list; the list now names the missed reservation and the overdue invoice.
- **Overview (section 1):** a certification no longer has a validity period of 365 days.
- **Tests and documentation (section 15):** tests and documentation no longer expect or describe expiry. Tests that relied on expiry now check that a certification issued long before a reservation date is still accepted.

## What stays as it is

Courses, enrollments, completion, revocation, the reservation and check-in rules apart from expiry, refusal codes and their order, and every other area are unchanged.
