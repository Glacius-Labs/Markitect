# Makerspace change C2: safety lockouts

Applies to the requirements after C1. Section numbers refer to [w0.md](w0.md).

## What changes

Until now an incident is only a record. From this change on, staff can use an open incident to lock its machine for a time window. A lockout cancels the reservations that have not started being used in that window, gives each affected member a credit, prevents new reservations in the window and is excluded from the machine's available time in the utilization report.

### Lockouts

A lockout belongs to one incident and locks that incident's machine for an interval `[start, end)`. Like maintenance windows, lockouts may use any minute, are not limited to opening hours and may overlap each other and maintenance windows. An incident can have several lockouts. A lockout cannot be changed, cancelled or ended early; resolving its incident does not end it.

New record, added to section 5:

| Record | Fields |
| --- | --- |
| lockout | `lockoutId`, `incidentId`, `machineId`, `start`, `end`, `cancelledReservationIds` (the reservations this lockout cancelled, in creation order) |

Lockout identifiers are generated like the others in section 2.6: `lock-1`, `lock-2`, … with their own counter.

### New operation `lockMachine` (Safety)

Mutation. Locks the machine of an open incident.

- Arguments: `incidentId` (identifier, an existing incident), `start` (timestamp), `end` (timestamp).
- Refusals, in this order: `invalid_state` when the incident is resolved; `invalid_interval` when `end` is not after `start`; `starts_in_past` when `start` is before the request time.
- Effect:
  1. A lockout is created for the incident's machine.
  2. Every reservation of that machine whose reported status is `reserved` and whose interval overlaps the lockout becomes `cancelled`. These are the reservations of the window that are not yet used: not checked in and not missed. This includes a reservation whose start has already passed but which is not yet checked in and not yet missed. Reservations that are checked in, completed, missed or already cancelled are not changed.
  3. For each reservation cancelled this way, the reservation's member receives a lockout credit of 1000 cents: the member's credit balance increases by 1000. This happens whatever the member's standing.
- Result: `lockout` and `cancelledReservations`, the reservations cancelled by this request (after the change), in creation order.
- Operation index row: `lockMachine` | Safety | mutation | member none | staff all | admin all | audit `target` the new `lockoutId`.

The request writes exactly one audit entry, for `lockMachine`. The cancellations and credits are part of that request and have no entries of their own.

### Changes to existing operations and rules

- **Reservations (section 9.1):** a new reservation rule follows rule 5: no lockout of the machine overlaps the slot. `createReservation` gets the refusal `machine_locked` when a lockout of the machine overlaps the slot. It is checked after `machine_in_maintenance` and before `machine_booked`.
- **Machine schedule:** `getMachineSchedule` gets a new result field `lockouts`: the machine's lockouts that overlap the interval from `00:00` of the date to `00:00` of the next date, ordered by `start` ascending, then creation order.
- **Utilization report (section 13):** rows and `totals` get a new field `lockoutMinutes`: the minutes of the range that lie inside opening hours and inside at least one lockout of the machine, overlapping lockouts counting once. `availableMinutes` becomes `openMinutes` minus the minutes of the range that lie inside opening hours and inside at least one scheduled maintenance window or lockout of the machine, each minute counted once. `maintenanceMinutes` keeps its definition, so a minute covered by both maintenance and a lockout appears in both `maintenanceMinutes` and `lockoutMinutes` but is subtracted from `availableMinutes` once. `totals.lockoutMinutes` is the sum over the rows, and `totals.availableMinutes` is still the sum of the rows' `availableMinutes`. `usedMinutes` keeps its definition: the lockout excludes time from the available minutes, not from the used minutes. A lockout never interrupts a checked-in session: a session already checked in when the lockout is created, and a session that overruns its reservation's end into a lockout, both keep metering until check-out, and their metered minutes count in full, also those inside the lockout.
- **Revenue report (section 13):** `creditsIssuedCents` also includes lockout credits whose `lockMachine` request time lies in the month.
- **Money (section 4.3):** the credit balance also increases by lockout credits.
- **Refusal codes (section 3.2):** new code `machine_locked`: a lockout of the machine overlaps the reservation.
- **Order of listed results (section 4.5):** new rows: `getMachineSchedule` → `lockouts`, by `start` ascending, then creation order; `lockMachine` → `cancelledReservations` and a lockout's `cancelledReservationIds`, in creation order.
- **Safety (section 12):** the sentence "In this delivery an incident is a record only: it does not block reservations or use" no longer applies. An incident itself still blocks nothing; only its lockouts do.
- **Overview (section 1):** the first cross-area rule also requires that no lockout of the machine overlaps the slot. The Safety bullet also says that staff can lock a machine after an incident.

## What stays as it is

- `cancelReservation` by a member, staff or admin gives no credit, as before.
- Maintenance windows, their rules and `reservation_conflict` are unchanged. Scheduling maintenance that overlaps a lockout is allowed, and a lockout never causes `reservation_conflict`.
- Check-in, check-out and metering are unchanged.
- Reporting and resolving incidents, the incident record and `listIncidents` are unchanged.
- C1's audit of `forbidden` refusals applies to `lockMachine` like any other operation.
- All other operations, codes, orders and permissions are unchanged.
