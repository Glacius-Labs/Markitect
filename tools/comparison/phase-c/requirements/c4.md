# Makerspace change C4: steward role

Applies to the requirements after C3. Section numbers refer to [w0.md](w0.md).

## What changes

A new role, `steward`, looks after the machines and safety on the workshop floor. A steward handles maintenance and incidents and has no access to billing.

- **Roles (section 4.1):** a user's role is now one of `member`, `staff`, `admin` or `steward`. Like every role, it is fixed at registration.
- **Registration:** `registerUser` accepts `role` from the enumeration `staff`, `admin` or `steward`. A steward is a user, not a member: a steward's identifier does not name a member, and a steward has no membership, invoices or credit.
- **Permissions:** the operation index (section 14) gets a Steward column. A steward has permission "all" for exactly these operations:

  | Operation | Steward |
  | --- | --- |
  | `scheduleMaintenance` | all |
  | `cancelMaintenance` | all |
  | `reportIncident` | all |
  | `resolveIncident` | all |
  | `listIncidents` | all |
  | `lockMachine` | all |
  | `listMachines` | all |
  | `getMachineSchedule` | all |
  | `listMaterials` | all |

  For every other operation a steward's permission is "none", and the request is refused with `forbidden` at check 4 of section 3.1. In particular a steward may not use any billing operation (`generateInvoices`, `listInvoices`, `recordPayment`, `issueCredit`, `getAccount`) or `revenueReport`, and also not `utilizationReport`, `listAuditEntries`, `registerUser`, any member, training, booking or materials operation not in the table, or the catalog operations `addFamily`, `addMachine` and `setMachineRate`.
- **Audit:** a steward's accepted mutations are recorded like everyone's, with `actorRole` `steward`. A steward's `forbidden` refusals are recorded as C1 states, also with `actorRole` `steward`.
- **Incidents:** when a steward reports an incident, `reportedBy` is the steward's identifier.

## What stays as it is

The permissions of members, staff and admins, all operation rules, records, refusal codes and their order are unchanged.
