# Credit

**Credit.** Each member has a credit balance, initially 0. It increases by overpayments, by credits an admin issues as a goodwill gesture and by lockout credits. Credit is applied automatically to invoices generated later, as [[billing/issuing-invoices|issuing invoices]] describes in step 5; it is never applied to an invoice that already exists and is never paid out.
