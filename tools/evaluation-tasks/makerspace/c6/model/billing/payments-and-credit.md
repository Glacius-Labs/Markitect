# Payments and credit

A [[billing/payment]] pays one invoice. The part of a payment that exceeds the invoice's balance becomes credit on the member's account. Admins can also issue credit as a goodwill gesture, and a lockout gives each member whose reservation it cancels a lockout credit. Credit is applied automatically to the next invoices generated; it is never applied to an invoice that already exists.
