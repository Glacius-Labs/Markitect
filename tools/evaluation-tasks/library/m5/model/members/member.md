# Member

A registered member of the [[project|library]] has a unique stable identifier and a nonempty name. Different members may have the same name. Identifier and name follow [[inputs]] and remain unchanged after registration. Members have no status: the library does not suspend members, and every registered member may borrow according to [[members/eligibility]]. This describes the member concept; it does not pre-create any live members.
