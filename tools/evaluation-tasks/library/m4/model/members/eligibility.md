# Eligibility

Only a registered active [[members/member]] may start a new loan or renew a loan. Suspension prevents new borrowing and renewal but preserves every existing loan, including its due date and open state. It does not prevent returning a loan. Restoring active status removes the suspension restriction; all other borrowing and renewal conditions still apply.

Lending must enforce this membership rule when admitting a new loan or a renewal, rather than relying on membership status being checked somewhere else.
