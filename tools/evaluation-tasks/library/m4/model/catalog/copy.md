# Copy

A copy is one physical book in the [[project|library]], with a unique stable identifier, a nonempty title under [[inputs]] and a loan class, either standard or short. Different copies may have the same title and are borrowed independently. Identifier, title and loan class remain unchanged after registration. The loan class selects the loan period under the loan policy of lending. There is no separate title or edition catalogue. The copy's current availability follows [[catalog/availability]].
