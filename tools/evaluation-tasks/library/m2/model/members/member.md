# Member

A registered member of the [[project|library]] has a unique stable identifier, a nonempty name and a status, active or suspended. Different members may have the same name. Identifier and name follow [[inputs]] and remain unchanged after registration. Registration creates an active member. The status controls borrowing according to [[members/eligibility]]. This describes the member concept; it does not pre-create any live members.
