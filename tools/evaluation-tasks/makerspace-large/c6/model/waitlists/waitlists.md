# Waitlists

A course that has no free place can have a waiting list. The list holds at most one [[waitlists/waiting-entry]] per member, in the order in which the members joined. Joining puts an entry at the end. A member who leaves and joins again is at the end. An entry leaves the list only when the member leaves it or a promotion enrolls the member ([[waitlists/waitlist-promotion]]).

A waiting list is not tied to the course being full: places that become free stay free until a promotion fills them, and a member can enroll directly in a free place. Enrolling directly does not remove the member's entry; a promotion then skips it.
