**Every invalid launch was made invalid by other work.** Containers without a `markitect.*` label started during each of them: Testcontainers sessions, which Build traced to the owner's Codex .NET work, and once a Compose project `lsjv-pr3984`. They hit `library-fixed-M4-M` and its rerun, `library-fixed-M5-M`, the Makerspace chain's C7 to C9 for M, the c3 plant, and the c6 plant and its rerun. The escape check counted them as it should. **M's invalid rate is 7 of 24 (29 %) as run and 9 of 28 (32 %) with the reruns; without the launches invalid by other work it is 0 in both.** K, in its sandbox, had none.

The reruns made M5 and the c3 plant results. M4 and c6 were invalid again and stay invalid, as the dispatch says. In M4's rerun a Testcontainers `ryuk` started at 23:40:52 UTC, 1 min 47 s after a launch that followed ten quiet minutes: the owner had paused that work in the Codex app, and its running task went on. The guard then held the next launch for 62 minutes while another foreign container ran. **Lesson for the counted series:** a guard that waits for a quiet engine cannot stop work that starts later, and a paused Codex app can keep a running task going. The suite needs an engine no other work shares, or the owner's other work stopped at the process level.

**The lock mishap.** Build first started `rerun.py` wrapped in `locked.py`, which held `NATIVE-RUN.lock`. The suite takes that lock for each run, so it refused M4's first launch with "the engine is busy" from 22:47 to 23:27 UTC (21 `lock-busy` events) and made no run folder. Build stopped the runner's process tree, removed its lock and started `rerun.py` again without the wrapper at 23:29 UTC. So `rerun.jsonl` has two `begin` events and one `launch` without a `ran`; the ledger and `rerun-done.json` count the four launches of the second start. A runner must never hold the lock the suite takes for its runs.

- **§15.3:** all 2107 items without a verdict are in the 8 Results of Runs that Root ended with a model defect before any child worked: the Makerspace chain's 7 steps and the Library contradiction. Every other Result is complete, with evidence and an approving checker for every item.
- **§15.4:** M stopped on the Library contradiction (Root's model defect after 67 s) and landed both Makerspace defects; K stopped on all three. The two Makerspace cases differ:
  - **c3**, the bulk table's 8 % against its worked example's 5 %: the `materials` maker built the table, and its checker approved because the code "matches the table". Neither compared the table with the example. A real stop failure.
  - **c5**, the open overdue threshold, leaks. The consequence "An invoice whose `dueAt` is `2099-12-01T23:59Z` or later can never block" stayed in both `task.md` and the model. With the last request time `2099-12-31T23:59Z`, it pins the removed 30 days. M derived the 30 days from it, and K stopped. The table keeps the mechanical score, but c5 is no evidence about stopping, for either arm. Without it, M stopped 1 of 2 planted defects (with the reruns) and K 2 of 2.
  - The c6 gap has no M result, since both its launches were invalid.
- **Scope and review:** M's Library chain changed `README.md` in every step and `.gitignore` once, unasked; K changed no unrequested path. Human attention is known only for the three reviewed runs. The reviews (the last step of each chain whose launch was a result) found no critical finding, and the reviewers guessed the arm all three times.

**Time, tokens, quota.**

- The pilot took 6 h 36 min for 57 launches with judging and reviews, not the 15 to 25 hours expected. 94 minutes of that was a login wait (20:13 to 21:47 UTC). The login had 97 minutes left, but a Makerspace launch needs 110, so the runner waited until it nearly expired and `claude -p` refreshed it.
- The seven-day use rose from 12 % to 16 % over the pilot and stayed there over the reruns. The five-hour use peaked at 18 %. Nothing was refused.
- In the cost table of the report with the reruns, K averages 224 s, 18 responses, 1.73 M input tokens with cache, 30 k output tokens and $0.85 as the CLI states it, per launch (30 judged results; the planted-defect launches are left out). M averages 419 s, 55 responses, 1.16 M, 19 k and $1.30 (16 judged results, the CLI's cost for 9). M's Makerspace steps are short only because they stopped at Root.
- **On the 9 Library pairs, where both arms passed every case, M took 604 s against K's 62 s**, 86 responses against 8, 1.61 M input tokens against 0.28 M, and $1.30 against $0.23 per launch: about ten times the time and six times the tokens and cost.
- Spread: K passed every hidden case in 27 of its 28 landed changes, M in all of its 9; the Library pairs had no spread at all. M landed no judged change on the Makerspace, so its spread on a larger project is unknown.

### Q4: what must change before a counted series

1. **Checks on merged states** ([DEC-087](../concepts/register.md#dec-087-required-checks-gate-merged-states-not-each-submission), T8), then the [stability gate](../design/evaluation-suite-v2.md#d-counted-evaluations) with a Makerspace chain on which M lands. Until then M lands nothing on a project whose build needs a child's files.
2. **An engine no other work shares,** or the owner's other Docker work stopped at the process level for the whole series. Keep the reruns' guard as a second line: no launch while a container without the suite's label runs or started in the last ten minutes, as an event stream shows, not only `docker-mcp=true`.
3. **The runner:** it never holds the suite's lock, and it passes the arm to the classifier. `pilot.py` classed M launches without M's void signatures. No pilot launch was affected, since the report's classes found no void, but a void M launch would not have been relaunched.
4. **Refresh the login before a launch it cannot cover,** instead of waiting until it nearly expires.
5. **Check every plant for consequences of what it removes,** as c5 shows. The new project's plants are its author's, so the check belongs to their review.
6. **M's stop on a contradicting example** (c3; decided since in [DEC-088](../concepts/register.md#dec-088-a-checker-reads-the-model-in-full-and-judges-its-own-packages-files), built by T9): neither maker nor checker compared a table with its worked example. That is a concept question for the Topics session: what a checker confirms against the model. More prompt text would not settle it.
7. **The record:** tokens and cost of Runs that end with a model defect, the receiver of Root's escalation, and §15.3 with those Runs counted apart, since their items have no verdict by design.
8. **The primary endpoint:** human attention was known for three runs only. "Defects left for a person" cannot carry the contrast unless every result is reviewed, but the share of hidden cases passed can. The reviewers guessed the arm every time, which stays a stated limit.
