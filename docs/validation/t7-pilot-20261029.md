# T7 pilot: the 2.0 arm and K on the Library and Makerspace series — 29 October 2026

The uncounted pilot of step T7 of the [delivery plan](../implementation-plan.md#concept-20-build), as section D of the [suite design](../design/evaluation-suite-v2.md#d-counted-evaluations) describes it, in the scope the owner chose: the concept 2.0 arm (M) and the baseline K on the Library and Makerspace series, once each, natively on Windows. It calibrates time, tokens, quota, spread and the void rate, and finds problems in either arm or the suite. **Pilot data never counts.** It runs under the owner's standing run approval of 28 October 2026 ([build workstream, Decisions](../workstreams/build.md)); the counted series needs its own protocol.

**Status: started on 29 October 2026, on `main` at `4364d77c`.** The results follow when it ends.
