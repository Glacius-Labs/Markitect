# Factual consistency

This page documented `Project.spec.consistency` assertions from the retired Project/Domain line. The historical behavior and contradictory fixture are preserved in the immutable [v0.14.1 guide](https://github.com/Glacius-Labs/Markitect/blob/v0.14.1/docs/consistency.md) and [source tree](https://github.com/Glacius-Labs/Markitect/tree/v0.14.1).

Current model-first Markitect does not infer factual consistency from adopting-project content. A project's Managers remain responsible for maintaining their declared knowledge and deciding what evidence warrants an edit; see [Project workflow](project-workflow.md).
