## 2026-10-09 — initial positioning note (assistant interpretation)

**Working identity:** Markitect helps people define a project's intended world in one authoritative model and organize delegated work to realize and check that intent.

**Problem framing:** As projects grow, people and agents can lose track of obligations, affected artifacts and cross-cutting consequences. Manual synchronization and broad review consume human attention.

**Proposed approach:** Make project intent, ownership and relevant relationships explicit; derive bounded work from changes; check candidate representations against the stated intent and declared evidence.

**Desired effect, still unproven:** Fewer forgotten obligations and contradictory representations, with less routine supervision and more attention available for architecture and unresolved decisions. Time and cost savings are additional hypotheses.

**Audience hypothesis, not selected:** Technical owners and maintainers of growing projects that already rely on coding agents. The fit and willingness to maintain a model remain open.

**Candidate public narrative:** „Du definierst, was dein Projekt sein soll. Markitect strukturiert die Absicht, ordnet die betroffene Arbeit zu und unterstützt ihre Umsetzung und Prüfung.“

The intended idea is that one maintained canonical source should guide reliable implementation while keeping still-applicable obligations in force. Structured modeling is expected to make ongoing work easier and consistency more sustainable; completeness, economic benefit and successful autonomous realization are not established claims.

**Open product question:** How much binding modeling is needed to identify change consequences, while leaving implementation details to delegates and keeping model upkeep sustainable?

**Marketing interpretation:** „Dauerhaft wirksam“ emphasizes the continuing force of a specification. „Das ganze betroffene Projekt“ communicates broad impact, but can be read as a completeness promise. Both remain candidate language for later review.

The aim is high agent autonomy and trust without making the human the bottleneck for model maintenance and conflict resolution. President, briefings, ministries, courts, legislature, judiciary and executive are a possible explanatory metaphor, not approved public branding.

**Status distinctions:** The contribution refers to an earlier, relatively immature implementation in a branch; that is not evidence of current capability. Keep that branch state, today's product behavior and the desired future operating model separate in any later narrative. The expected benefit and feasibility are explicitly open questions to investigate. Fine-grained planning remains deferred as recorded above.

**Marketing interpretation:** The strongest underlying promise is human attention shifting from supervising every implementation to receiving decisions and escalations that matter. The Government metaphor can make authority and review roles vivid, but carries its own conceptual weight; whether it clarifies the product for intended users is unresolved.
