## How to read these sources

The current [product vision](../vision.md) owns the thesis and human/agent responsibilities. [Architecture](../architecture.md) and the [project workflow](../project-workflow.md) describe current technical behavior, the [delivery plan](../implementation-plan.md) owns rebuild scope, and the [production assessment](../history/distribution/production-assessment.md) records published release evidence. Archived vision and positioning proposals preserve origin and alternatives; their recommendations do not establish current behavior or measured benefits.

Received 2026-10-03: [parallel-development coordinator bundle](sources/parallel-development/README.md). Its coordinator prompt was invoked for preparation only. Supporting adoption UX, risks and adapter proposals remain design input; illustrative schemas/commands are not shipped features. Adopted decisions live in [development coordination](../development/README.md) and the existing [constitution](https://github.com/Glacius-Labs/Markitect/blob/1f4a1ede214d05fd2fee615b3905207df7702426/docs/engineering-constitution.md). The workstream packages derived from it were retired on 10 October 2026; git history keeps them.

The proposals contain product claims, design options, recommendations and imperatives. Their market claims and expected benefits have not been independently verified. Use [Measurement](../measurement.md) and the scoped [validation records](../validation/README.md) to assess evidence, including negative and inconclusive findings. The [refinement history](https://github.com/Glacius-Labs/Markitect/blob/1f4a1ede214d05fd2fee615b3905207df7702426/docs/refinement.md), [historical engineering plan](https://github.com/Glacius-Labs/Markitect/blob/1f4a1ede214d05fd2fee615b3905207df7702426/docs/canonical-engineering-plan.md) and [history map](../history/README.md) retain earlier adopted scope without reviving it as the current architecture.
