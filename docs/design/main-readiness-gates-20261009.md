Root decision, 9 October 2026, under the human's explicit request in Monitor to decide the P09/P10 boundary.

Comparative Playground Case Studies are a separate evaluation. They never gate the current Main integration. Three broad native acceptance journeys are also more preparation than Main needs. Replace that requirement with one small genuine product smoke covering the new execution mechanisms, supported by meaningful regression tests and required platform gates. The generous runtime limits remain upper bounds, not target durations.

## Before Main

- Coherent integrated source, build/install/onboarding and current documentation.
- Relevant regressions for compiler diagnostics, typed inputs, privacy/ownership/freshness, Git add/delete/rename/binary changes, failure preservation, cleanup, retry IDs and recovery.
- Required Linux and Windows CI/PR checks on the final candidate and independent review with material defects resolved.
- One real Codex/Luna High product smoke on that candidate: ordinary setup and small Work Item/model through Run, independent Review/Verify and guarded Apply. Observe two small independent Managers overlap, one genuine fresh helper and persisted original-turn/workspace recovery without replay. A compact shared journey may cover all these mechanisms; no comparative harness, private evaluator, large domain application or artificial failure matrix is needed. Actual encountered diagnostics are repaired through the normal agent-owned workflow; broader fault injection stays in regression tests or later exploratory work.

Critical normal-work defects and concretely unresolved owned lifecycle/ownership block Main. Missing usage telemetry alone does not. Retain partial-accounting limits rather than inventing complete isolation or hard native-helper guarantees. Real smoke evidence, deterministic fixtures, CI and independent review have distinct claims.

## After Main

Broad native error/repair, rename/delete and recovery journeys and larger parallel or long-running projects belong to subsequent product exploration. Comparative Conventional versus Markitect and KG evaluation belong to the separately authorized Playground research. Neither is automatically started by Main merge. Product checks do not establish comparative superiority or a release decision.
