# Review cases are recorded before the work they assess (18 October 2026)

Source of [DEC-049](../register.md#dec-049-review-cases-are-recorded-before-the-work-they-assess).

## Evidence

In bound run R2-A1-08 (Main a9f62311), 6 of 11 fresh case sets were rejected on their first review. The rejections named scenarios that only the case set specified, such as exact due dates or a large name through the command. The Manager could not know them before the review. Reviews against cases the Manager had already seen passed 7 times out of 8. That comparison is confounded, because those reviews followed rework.

The journals of R2-A1-04 to R2-A1-08 show where the time goes. Agent steps take about 96 % of the wall time and run one after another. Reviews make up about 40 % of all steps, two steps per submission: 30 of 73 in R2-A1-08.

The expectations step sees only the model, the purpose, the task's open findings and the owner's decisions. None of these depend on a candidate. Recording the cases before the writing step therefore changes nothing that step can see. It lets the Manager build against the cases, and most reviews keep only their candidate step.

Recording a reviewer step before a work step exposed an ordering gap. A parent that asks a child and submits in the same step relied on a review delay. Without that delay, its pass could be accepted before the child answered. Reply steps now come first among items that become admissible together.

## Owner instruction

On the same day the owner asked the Architect to speed the runs up and to say what he would change in the concept:

> Ausserdem schaue mal, ob du den Prozess verbessern oder verschnellern kannst, sodass die Läufe nicht so ewig dauern. [...] Ausserdem schaue auch ob du etwas am Konzept ändern würdest. Wir lösen die Probleme nicht durch bessere Agenten.

The owner had earlier authorized changes the Architect judges sensible:

> Ja setze alles um was du für sinnvoll hälst und schaue auch, ob du das ganze etwas beschleunigen kannst.
