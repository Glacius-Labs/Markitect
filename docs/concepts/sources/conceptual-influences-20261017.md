## Owner statement — English translation

The following ideas contributed to Markitect's development and conception:

- The four-eyes principle.
- Areas of responsibility.
- Delegation and escalation.
- Divide and conquer: split a problem into its parts and put them back together.
- Models and projections: the software project as a projection of a canonical model, or realization of a worldview.
- Control through good planning, system and structure, complementing AI agents: Trust the Process.
- Stakeholders, steering through incentives, and different perspectives on the same subject.
- A type system for consistency: typed versus dynamic languages; a compiler catches many things at compile time instead of runtime.
- Many inexpensive subagents working systematically together, such as Luna instead of Astra, or Sonnet or Haiku instead of Fable, rather than one God Agent; the analogy is a God Class.
- Abstraction and need to know for cleaner context; coordinate agents with broad knowledge and agents with deep knowledge.
- Doc-driven development, spec-driven development, Single Source of Truth, Clean Architecture, the Dependency Rule, software architecture lessons and programming in Markdown, now YAML. The original naming explanation was "Markitect = Markdown Arcitect".
