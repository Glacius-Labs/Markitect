# Rule

Something that holds and that someone could get wrong if it were not written down, such as a lending period or a limit. Take it for each statement that governs other documents. Take a [decision](decision.md) instead for a choice with its reason and alternatives, and a [concept](concept.md)'s own section "Rules" for what holds for that one concept alone.

## Applies to
What it governs, as links. A link to a folder's README means everything in that folder. Example: `[Loans](../lending/README.md)`.

## Rule
What holds, in a few sentences. Example: `A member has at most five open loans. A sixth is refused until one is returned.`

## Responsible (optional)
Type: [Role](role.md)

The role that watches over it, as a link. Example: `[Librarian](../team/librarian.md)`.

## Example (optional)
A short case that shows it. An example illustrates and fixes nothing. Example: `Ada holds five loans and asks for a sixth; the desk refuses.`
