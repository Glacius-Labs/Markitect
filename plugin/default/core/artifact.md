# Artifact

Something that is built for someone to use: a page, a view, a handbook, an API, a client, a plugin. Whatever its form, the same things matter: whom it is for and what it offers them. Take it for each such thing as a whole. The document describes it; the thing itself is built from it, and its form may change without the description changing.

## For
Who uses it, as links to roles or in a few words, and the one question it answers for them. Example: `Members: which of my loans are due?`

## Offers
What it offers, in the order the user meets it, one per line: the name and what it shows or does. Example: `1. Due soon: the loans due within a week, one line each.`

## Form (optional)
The form it takes, where that is settled: a page, a document, an API, and what holds for that form. Example: `One page, readable on a phone.`
