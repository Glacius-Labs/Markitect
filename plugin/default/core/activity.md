# Activity

Something that is done again and again: planning a story, building it, a triage, a meeting, creating a feature. Take it for every piece of work that comes up often enough to be described once. The text says how it is done, as one would tell a colleague; a skill is built from it. An activity may name other activities in its text, where it uses or starts them, and what arises in it: a report, a transcript, a plan.

## Participants
Type: [Role](role.md)

Who takes part, each a link to a [Role](role.md), with a few words on what that role does in it where several take part. One role does it alone; several do it together, and the list stands here in full. Who actually took part stands in the report.

## Trigger
What starts it: an event, such as `a story is ready`, or a rhythm for an activity that returns, such as `daily` or `weekly, on Mondays at 09:30`. An activity with a rhythm is due when its last run lies further back than the rhythm says.

## Steps (optional)
How it is done, as a numbered list in plain words. Every step says one thing that someone does. What is not done, or what only has to be kept in mind, is no step: it stands under Notes.

## Result (optional)
What holds when it is done, and what it leaves behind.

## Notes (optional)
What to keep in mind that is no step: what is not done here, what holds throughout, a limit, a hint. A note that returns in several activities is a rule and belongs there.
