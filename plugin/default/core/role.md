# Role

Someone who acts and is named by what they usually do, such as the librarian. Take it for each kind of actor whom the model lets do or decide something. The text before the first section says in a sentence or two what the role is there for, and whether it works with a person or alone. A role describes the usual way of working, not a permission. What a role does and answers for is not written here: it does every activity that names it among its Participants, and it answers for every module and rule that names it under Responsible. Who fills a role stands with the [actor](actor.md), a person or an agent.

## Reports to (optional)
Type: [Role](role.md)

The role it answers to, as a link to a [Role](role.md). Example: `[Head librarian](head-librarian.md)`.
