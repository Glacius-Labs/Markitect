# Concept

Something the project knows and names, with a meaning and rules of its own, such as a loan or a copy of a book. Take it for each thing the system, the business or the way of working manages. Take a [term](term.md) instead for a word that only needs a meaning, and a [rule](rule.md) for a statement that holds across several concepts.

## Definition
What it is, in one or two sentences. Example: `A loan lets one member keep one copy for a fixed period.`

## Consists of (optional)
What it is made of, one per line: a link where that is a concept of its own, else its name and what it holds. Example: `[Member](member.md)`, `Due date: the day the copy must be back.`

## Lifecycle (optional)
The states it passes through and what moves it from one to the next. Example: `open, then returned when the copy is handed back, or overdue after the due date.`

## Rules (optional)
What always holds for it, in short sentences. Example: `A copy is on loan to at most one member at a time.`
