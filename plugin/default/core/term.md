# Term

A word with a fixed meaning in this project, such as "due date". Take it for each word of the field that readers could understand in two ways. Take a [concept](concept.md) instead for a thing with parts, states or rules of its own.

## Meaning
What the word means here, in one or two sentences. Example: `The day by which a borrowed copy must be back, counted from the day of the loan.`
