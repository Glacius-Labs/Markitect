# Where the work lies

Type: [Rule](../../core/rule.md)

## Applies to
[The model](../../README.md)

## Rule
The work lies in the model, in modules of its own beside the modules that say what should hold: [plan](../../plan/README.md), with the features and stories, cut like the modules of the model; [findings](../../findings/README.md); [reports](../../reports/README.md), with one module per role and per watch; [briefings](../../briefings/README.md), with one module per recurring meeting; and [drops](../../drops/README.md), with the dropped material. Reports and drops together are the context: what happened; it is information only, demands nothing, and stays. These modules name no realization, so a change in them never counts as a change to what should hold. They refer to what should hold, a story to the places it changes, a finding to the module it concerns; nothing that says what should hold refers to them. What each holds: [report](../observing/report.md), [finding](../triage/finding.md), [feature](../plan/feature.md), [story](../plan/story.md) and [briefing](../briefing/briefing.md).
