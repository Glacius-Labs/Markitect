# The dropped material

Type: [Watch](watch.md)

State what the material says that people and agents dropped into the context: transcripts, messages and notes. For each drop, the relevant statements, bundled by theme, each with its speaker and its place: file, time mark and speaker. What in a transcript is only noise, or is about something else, stays out. A meeting's transcript becomes a report of the meeting's statements by theme.

## Participants
[Observer](../team/observer.md)

## Trigger
A new drop lies in the context: due in every run that finds one.

## Steps
[Observe](observe.md)

## Result
A report with the statements, or only the checkpoint when there was nothing to state.

## Source
The drops in the context and the raw material kept beside them, read as files in the repository; never the reports of other watches, and never the reports left at the end of sessions, which the Triager reads directly. Observed to: the last drop read.

## Never in the report
Customer data and secrets; where a statement cannot be shown without them, only the reference stands. The wording of the raw material is never changed or smoothed where it counts.
