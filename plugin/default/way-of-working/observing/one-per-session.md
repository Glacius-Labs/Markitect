# One per session

Type: [Rule](../../core/rule.md)

## Applies to
[Every role](../team/README.md), [Report](report.md)

## Rule
Every session and every run lays one short [report](report.md) into the module of reports of its role at its end, every session that works on a plan item included: planning, building, analyzing, accepting. A run of a watch that observed nothing writes no report, only its checkpoint. A report with nothing noteworthy in it gives the Triager no finding.
