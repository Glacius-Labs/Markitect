# Drop what you notice

Type: [Rule](../../core/rule.md)

## Applies to
[Every role](../team/README.md)

## Rule
Whatever strikes an agent outside its task, it drops into the context with [capture](capture.md) and carries on: a smell, a bug, a risk, a contradiction between documents, a merge conflict, a workaround it took, a step of the way of working that got in its way. One drop per observation: what, where, and what it was doing. It does not investigate, fix or classify it. Before its session ends, it drops what it has not dropped yet. What belongs to its own task it does and does not drop. This holds for everyone, Observer, Triager, Builder and Reviewer included. The drop is raw material: the watch on the dropped material makes the report, the Triager the finding; when something repeats, it shows at its smell story.
