# Capture

Type: [Activity](../../core/activity.md)

## Trigger
A person says something or hands over material, such as a transcript, or whoever is at work notices something outside their task ([drop what you notice](drop-what-you-notice.md)). Everyone does it, at any time, inside the session they are in.

## Steps
1. Take what the person says or hands over, or what was noticed. One drop per observation.
2. Keep raw material that lies nowhere else, transcripts, conversations, messages, unchanged and hidden beside the drops. Do not copy what lies elsewhere, such as logs or monitoring; point to it.
3. Write the drop into the context: the wording unchanged, with what only this session knows right now: who, when, and what was being worked on. What a person decided is marked as a decision: who decided, in which words.
4. For a [deviation](../deviations/course.md), write who, what, which role would be usual, the level, and the reason in the person's words.
5. For a [merge conflict](../building/merge-conflicts.md), write which files and which two stories.
6. Never take customer data or secrets into the repository ([what is critical](../deviations/critical.md)); raw material that holds them is not kept: the drop then says what the material says, in its wording, without them, and points to where the material lies.
7. Write straight to `main` ([straight to main](../work/straight-to-main.md)), confirm in one sentence, and carry on.

## Result
The drop lies in the context on `main`, in the wording it was said in; raw material that lies nowhere else is kept beside it. The watch on [the dropped material](watch-dropped-material.md) makes a report from it.

## Participants
Everyone: whoever notices something or is told something, in whichever role, human or agent.

## Notes
What was dropped is not investigated, fixed or classified here: that is the triage's. Customer data and secrets never go into the repository.
