# Critical stays on top until done

Type: [Rule](../../core/rule.md)

## Applies to
[The Briefer](../team/briefer.md), [the Assistant](../team/assistant.md)

## Rule
Critical is everything that carries `critical` and is still open, wherever it lies: an open finding as well as a story that arose from a critical finding. A story that arose from a critical finding is shown on top until it is `done` or `cancelled`. What is critical always goes to a human ([the types of findings](../triage/finding-types.md)), who decides what is to be done; what only a human may do becomes their own story ([commitments](../plan/commitments.md)). The finding is closed only when the stories that arose from that decision are `done` or `cancelled`: critical ends when the damage is dealt with, not when its cause is analysed.
