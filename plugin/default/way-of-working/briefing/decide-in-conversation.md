# People decide in conversation

Type: [Rule](../../core/rule.md)

## Applies to
[Meetings](meetings/README.md), [the Assistant](../team/assistant.md), [the Project manager](../team/project-manager.md)

## Rule
People decide in conversation, never through an approval, a form or a field: alone with their agent, or together, in a meeting or between them ([decide](decide.md)). What was decided lands in the context in the words it was said in, and the Project manager carries it out. Nobody has to approve what went to work without a decision; anyone may object.
