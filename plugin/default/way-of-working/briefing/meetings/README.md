# Meetings

The recurring meetings of this project, in which people decide in conversation ([decide in conversation](../decide-in-conversation.md)). A meeting decides; it writes no plan: one participant gives the transcript to their agent, it lands in the context, and the Project manager carries out what was decided.

## Contents
None yet: this module belongs to the project and starts empty.

## How to fill it
Write one [activity](../../../core/activity.md) per recurring meeting, or leave the module empty: the daily and the weekly briefing are written without any meeting ([when shown](../when-shown.md)). Its first paragraph says what is discussed there and which briefing it takes, such as `The weekly briefing: findings of importance normal and low`; its Participants name every role that takes part; its Trigger says how often and at what time, in the project's time zone, such as `weekly, on Mondays at 09:30`; its Result says what is decided when it ends. A meeting in which the round decides names [decide](../decide.md) in its steps and adds only what is its own. Who actually took part stands in the meeting's report. A meeting named `daily.md` or `weekly.md` takes that briefing; any other recurring meeting gets a briefing of its own. Meet only as much as needed: for planning, the state, ideas, problems and questions, and only about what is built, so that everyone knows roughly what a feature is. The [Briefer](../../team/briefer.md) writes each meeting's briefing in time, and [the Assistant](../../team/assistant.md) says on the morning of its day that it is due and that its briefing lies ready.
