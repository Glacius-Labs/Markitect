# Briefing

What reaches the humans: short, ordered by importance, one line per finding with a link to it. Nobody has to read more than the line. There is a daily and a weekly briefing, which follow from the importances and need no meeting, one that anyone can ask for at any time, and one for each further recurring [meeting](meetings/README.md). A briefing decides nothing: people decide in conversation, and the Project manager carries out what they decided. A block with nothing in it says `none`.

## Where we stand
A few lines so that everyone has the same picture before anything is decided: what landed since the last briefing, what is in progress and who works on it, what the run started, what is blocked, and for each feature in progress which of its stories are built and which are not yet. Example: `Landed: renewing a loan. In progress: due dates on the member's page (Builder). Blocked: fines, waits for the members' council.`

## Needs a decision
Type: [Finding](../triage/finding.md)

The [findings](../triage/finding.md) that are `pending` and wait for people, critical first, then by importance. Which importance goes into which briefing, [when shown](when-shown.md) says, and for a further meeting what that meeting covers. One line each: importance, kind, module, the finding's title as a link. Example: `1. [high] Requirement · Lending · Members want to renew online`.

## Decided for you
What was added to the model because it follows from what is decided, each with the place and the finding. A finding reverses it. Example: `Lending period: renewals follow the same period [finding]`.

## Went to work without a decision
The findings that went from `ready` into the plan since the last briefing, each with its story. Anyone can object. Example: `Bug · Lending · Due date shown in the wrong time zone → story, ready`.

## Decided yesterday
The decisions recorded since the last briefing, and what became of each. Example: `Idea "reading lists": accepted, now a feature in the module Catalog`.
