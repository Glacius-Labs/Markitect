# Decide

Type: [Activity](../../core/activity.md)

People go through what waits for them and decide, in conversation. It is the same whether one person decides alone with their agent or several decide together in a meeting.

## Participants
[Admin](../team/admin.md): decides; with several people, everyone in the round.
[Assistant](../team/assistant.md): when a person decides alone, drops what they decided.

## Trigger
A briefing lies ready with findings that need a decision; or a person takes up a finding with their agent, at any time.

## Steps
1. Start from where we stand: go through the summary at the top of the [briefing](briefing.md), what happened since the last time, what is in progress and what is blocked, until everyone has the same picture.
2. Go through what the briefing lists under Needs a decision, critical first, then by importance ([when shown](when-shown.md)).
3. Decide on each finding in conversation: take it over, reject it, park it, or name who answers it; when a story is given up, say also what becomes of its documents and of the stories that wait for it ([giving up a story](../plan/giving-up-a-story.md)); for a `critical` finding, the agent first goes through with the person what has to be stopped at once, what has to be cleaned up and who has to know, and notes what the person does right away as done; for an Unclassified finding, say what it is ([the types of findings](../triage/finding-types.md)).
4. Look at what went to work without a decision and at what was decided for you. Nobody has to approve it; whoever objects says so, and that is a decision like any other.
5. Get the decisions into the context, in the words they were said in ([capture](../observing/capture.md)). A person alone: the agent they talk with drops what they decided, naming each finding by its link. Several people: one participant gives the transcript to their agent.

## Result
Everyone has the same picture of where the project stands, and every finding that was taken up has a decision in the context, in the words it was said in. The [Project manager](../team/project-manager.md) carries the decisions out ([record](../recording/record.md)); until then the plan and the model are unchanged. That happens in the next [daily run](../day/daily-run.md), which a person may start at once.
