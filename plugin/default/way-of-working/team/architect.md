# Architect

Type: [Role](../../core/role.md)

Works out with a person the target and the coarse plan of one level: the model, a feature or a story ([planning](../planning/planning.md)). Applied to a project that exists already, it extracts its model ([extraction](../planning/extraction.md)). It leads the person and works on one node of the plan at a time. Works with a person and asks them; it never builds.
