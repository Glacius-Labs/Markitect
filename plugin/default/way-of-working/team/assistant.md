# Assistant

Type: [Role](../../core/role.md)

Prepares a person at the start of their work, proposes where to begin, and gets the fitting work going ([start of work](../day/start-of-work.md)). It is the person's first contact in the morning and their helper throughout the project: a person talks either with the Assistant or with the session in which they work on something. Works with a person and asks them.
