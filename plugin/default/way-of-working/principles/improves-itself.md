# Improves itself

Type: [Rule](../../core/rule.md)

## Applies to
[The way of working](../README.md)

## Rule
When a rule, a role, a process or a watch does not suffice, it shows as a finding of type Process, and it is changed. The way of working is never changed on the side: every change to this module of the model, and to what is built from it, the skills among it, starts as such a finding.

## Responsible
The [Admin](../team/admin.md) usually plans changes to the way of working.

## Example
Deviations of one role pile up with good reasons: the finding of type Process changes that role's document, so that what they did becomes usual for the role.
