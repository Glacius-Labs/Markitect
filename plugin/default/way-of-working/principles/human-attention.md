# Human attention is the currency

Type: [Rule](../../core/rule.md)

## Applies to
[The way of working](../README.md)

## Rule
Humans work on the model; agents bring it into the artifacts. Carrying out is cheap with agents, keeping humans in step is expensive. One question runs through everything: does it need a human?

## Example
A bug whose model is clear needs nobody: it goes into the plan and is built. An idea needs a human: it goes into the briefing.
