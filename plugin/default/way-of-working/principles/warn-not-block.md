# Warn, do not block

Type: [Rule](../../core/rule.md)

## Applies to
[The way of working](../README.md)

## Rule
The way of working supports and evens out; it keeps nobody from working. Nothing is forbidden: every role has a usual way of working, and every deviation from it has a level ([deviations](../deviations/README.md)).

## Example
In a feature someone else has claimed, usually only its owner writes. Another person who writes there is warned and asked why, not stopped.
