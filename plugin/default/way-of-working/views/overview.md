# Overview

Type: [Artifact](../../core/artifact.md)

## For
Everyone: where do we stand, who does what?

## Offers
1. Critical: everything critical that is not done ([critical until done](../briefing/critical-until-done.md)), one line each: the findings with `critical` that are `pending` or `ready`, and the stories of priority `critical` that are still open. The Assistant says it always, first.
2. People: for each [person](../team/README.md), their role, their own features, their stories `in progress` with who works on them, Builder or Analyst, and in `acceptance`. The Assistant says only what touches the person.
3. Modules: one line per module of the model: its features, its stories by state as bars, and how many stories belong to no feature. The Assistant says nothing of it.
4. Landed: what landed on `main` since the last briefing, and what was aborted. The Assistant says each in a sentence.
5. Briefing: the open findings as a number per importance, leading to the [Briefing](briefing.md) view. The Assistant says it as a number.
