# My work

Type: [Artifact](../../core/artifact.md)

## For
The [Admin](../team/admin.md): what waits for me, where do I start?

## Offers
1. Critical: as in the [Overview](overview.md). The Assistant says it always, first.
2. Decisions: the `pending` findings that concern my modules and features or ask me, by importance. The Assistant says those with `high`, else the number.
3. My features: for each feature I own its progress, and for each module I am responsible for the stories without a feature; below each its stories: aborted builds, `ready` (start the build), `draft` (plan it), `in progress` (Builder or Analyst), `acceptance`; from the plan and the reports of aborted builds. The Assistant says what waits for me.
4. Acceptance: the stories in `acceptance`, oldest first, bundled by feature; then the features that wait to be accepted as a whole. The Assistant says what waits for me.
5. My stories: the stories I do myself ([commitments](../plan/commitments.md)). The Assistant says them.
6. Way of working: the findings of type Process and Unclassified that are due to be shown ([when shown](../briefing/when-shown.md)); whether the daily run ran today; overdue watches. The Assistant says what waits for me.
7. Around me: what others do that touches me: dependencies of my stories, work of others in my features. The Assistant says it briefly.
8. Without decision: what went into the plan in my modules without a decision, and what was added to the model there because it follows from what is decided, open to objection. The Assistant says each in a sentence.
9. Proposal: one line from the Assistant, where to start.
