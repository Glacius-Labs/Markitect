# Views

What people see of the work: the views and the handbook, each described as what it offers and for whom, whatever form it takes: a page, a document or something else. Together the views answer in one minute: is anything burning, what waits for me, who does what. The page is built from these documents, as any page is built from its description; the Assistant says the top of the views in the morning, and the rest stays on the page to look up.

## What holds for every view
A view stores nothing: it shows the plan, the findings, the context and the briefings as they lie on `main`, and links to them, so everyone sees the same picture of the same state. The page is built locally from the work as it lies on `main` and is never checked in. It shows what is going on through the claims of those who work on an item. Until a project has built the page, [the Assistant](../team/assistant.md) tells what the views would show directly from the work, and no agent builds or rebuilds a page.

On purpose, no view shows code, diffs, review rounds or single test runs; the tasks of others, other than as a number at the story; the full text of findings and reports, other than their title line with a link; closed findings and history, figures or trends; the way of working itself; or the log of running sessions, since the story shows who works on it.

## How to fill it
A project with more roles adds a view per role: a document of type [Artifact](../../core/artifact.md) here, linked from this list. A view is changed through a finding of type Process.
