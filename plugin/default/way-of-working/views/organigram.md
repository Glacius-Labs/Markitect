# Organigram

Type: [Artifact](../../core/artifact.md)

## For
Everyone: who is there, what does each role answer for, and who answers to whom?

## Offers
1. Roles: every role of [the team](../team/README.md) with who fills it, a person or an agent with its AI model.
2. What each role does and answers for: the activities that name it among their Participants, and the modules and rules that name it under Responsible.
3. Lines: who reports to whom, as far as roles say so.

## Form
Computed from the team and from what names its roles; nobody keeps it by hand.
