# Report a smell, do not build around it

Type: [Rule](../../core/rule.md)

## Applies to
[The Builder](../team/builder.md), [the Reviewer](../team/reviewer.md)

## Rule
Clean and a little later is better than ugly and at once. A Builder who meets a smell reports it and does not build around it; it stays with its own story, or stops until the smell is gone. It neither fixes nor investigates the smell on the side.
