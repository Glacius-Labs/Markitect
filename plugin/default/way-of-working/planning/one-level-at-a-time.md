# One level at a time

Type: [Rule](../../core/rule.md)

## Applies to
[Planning](planning.md), [the Architect](../team/architect.md)

## Rule
Planning is the same on every level and is applied to one node of the tree; for the node's children the same role starts in a new session. The person works on one level at a time: they go down the stairs, leave everything else aside and see on the horizon only the level above. They go through the stories of a feature one after another, depth first. Once the plan of a story is secured, the next one can be planned while the first is built. With every piece of work the person learns everything relevant anew, from the model down, as if they knew nothing. A level that gains only one story is planned together with that story, in the same session: a wish that fits into one story costs one planning, not two.

## Example
A person plans the feature "Renew online", cuts three stories, and plans the first in a new session; while it is built, they plan the second.
