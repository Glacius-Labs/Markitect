# A finished planning reopens only after a finding

Type: [Rule](../../core/rule.md)

## Applies to
[Planning](planning.md)

## Rule
A finished planning is reopened only when it has failed while building: after an aborted build, whose report is reason enough, or after a finding that says so. A person reopens the story with the Architect. When nobody wants the story any more, that is decided like anything else, and the story is given up ([giving up a story](../plan/giving-up-a-story.md)).
