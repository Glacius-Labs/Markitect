# Planning

Type: [Activity](../../core/activity.md)

## Trigger
A person takes a level to work out: the model, a feature or a story. [The Assistant](../team/assistant.md) starts the Architect for it, in a session of its own, to which the person switches. A feature or a story is planned once it stands in the plan: an idea or a decision that is not there yet goes through the circle first, for which the Assistant offers the [daily run](../day/daily-run.md).

## Steps
1. **Claim.** The Architect claims the feature or story before it begins, for the person ([claiming](../plan/claiming.md)), and writes it down on `main`: for a feature it enters the person as Owner and sets the feature `in progress`; for a story it enters the person under Claimed by. The model has nothing to claim. When the session ends without step 11, it releases the story's claim itself and brings the findings it closed to `main` with it.
2. **Collect context.** The Architect gathers, before it says anything: what the level above says, the rules that hold for the module, what lies in the context about it, and what already exists, with a judgment for each building block: keep, clean up or new ([make room first](make-room-first.md)). It questions the model with the tool, `markitect show`, `markitect table` and `markitect topic`, and follows the links that have to do with the item, in both directions. It checks whether its assumptions about what the item builds on hold.
3. **Brief.** The Architect explains to the person, as if they knew nothing: what it is about, how it relates to the level above, which is left out for the model as a whole, and what the rules for the module say. It also retells what the context already holds about the item, such as what a meeting said, so that the person does not have to say it again. Always abstract, never details of the realization.
4. **Collect.** The person tells freely what comes to mind. The Architect proposes nothing yet; at most it asks back. Its proposal comes only when nothing more comes to the person.
5. **Order.** The Architect structures what was said. When the topic is large, its parts are gone through one by one, each by the same procedure, from step 2 on.
6. **Findings of the feature.** When planning in a feature, the Architect goes through the findings attached to it, those `ready` ones whose outcome links the feature, with its owner: it takes each up into a story, whose origin links the finding, or sets it aside with the reason, adds that below the finding's outcome and closes it ([finding](../triage/finding.md)). A `pending` finding that the planning answers on the way is decided there too, and the Architect drops that decision into the context.
7. **Summarize.** The Architect says in its own words what it has understood. The person checks whether they were understood right. The Architect writes what should hold into the model itself, not into the story: the changed documents, in its working folder, where the Reviewer of the next step reads them. In a feature's planning it puts what a later story builds into a document of its own, so that every story can name whole documents ([the model runs ahead](../plan/the-model-runs-ahead.md)).
8. **Check.** A fresh [Reviewer](../team/reviewer.md), started by the Architect, compares what the planning worked out, the change to the model, with every level above it, up to the model as a whole ([every level fits the levels above](../plan/every-level-fits.md)). When one does not fit, work on it continues.
9. **Plan.** The Architect plans on its own and tells abstractly how it means to realize it in principle, and names the stumbling blocks here. For a feature the plan is the cut into stories, with their order, their dependencies what belongs into the first version, and how the feature stays switched off until it is accepted ([off until accepted](../accepting/off-until-accepted.md)); for a story it is the tasks. For the model, the plan is the stories that carry the change, each in the plan of the module that holds the place it changes, so that it is built through a story like every other change. Every story it creates gets its priority ([priorities](../plan/priorities.md)) and its dependencies set explicitly ([dependencies](../plan/dependencies.md)) and names, under Realizes, every place in the model its files will realize and how the story changes it ([story](../plan/story.md)); a story cut in a feature's planning holds the places as far as they are known, completed when it is planned. A task only a human may do, such as an act in production, becomes a story of its own for that person ([commitments](../plan/commitments.md)), on which the others depend. Stories with fixed tasks are created with exactly those tasks ([fixed tasks](../plan/fixed-tasks.md)). For a story that adds a watch, [add a watch](../observing/add-a-watch.md) says what to ask and write. When the stories can only be cut with many dependencies, the Architect writes that down as coupling for the report at the end.
10. **Go.** The Architect gives a last summary. When the person says yes, that is their go. Nobody else approves the plan.
11. **Finish.** The planning is closed, in this order, inside the planning session:
    1. A fresh Reviewer checks the plan, and the changed documents of the model, against the target of its own level. When it does not fit, the planning goes on.
    2. The report on the course of the planning goes into the context ([one per session](../observing/one-per-session.md)), with any coupling the cut showed.
    3. A planned story is set to `ready` and its claim released ([claiming](../plan/claiming.md)), or to `done` when its planning left nothing to build, as when it only took documents out of the model; after a feature or the model was planned, the new stories stay `draft` until each is planned; their origin is the feature, or, for the model, the report of the planning, which may also create features, `open`; a feature that was planned keeps its owner and `in progress`.
    4. The change to the model and the plan go to `main` together, with the findings the planning closed ([the model runs ahead](../plan/the-model-runs-ahead.md)).
    5. The Architect tells the person that the plan is secured and the chat can be archived, and sends them back to [the Assistant](../team/assistant.md).

## Result
The model says what should hold at the planned level, and the change fits every level above; the change to the model and the plan are on `main`, and a planned story is `ready`. The findings attached to the feature are taken up or set aside, and closed. "Planned through" means: the model stands right.

## Participants
The person who plans and gives the go: usually whoever is responsible for the module, or whom a rule for the module names; by default the [Admin](../team/admin.md).
The [Architect](../team/architect.md) leads the conversation and writes; a fresh [Reviewer](../team/reviewer.md) checks in steps 8 and 11.

## Notes
When it is unclear how something should be, or an interface is missing, the Architect drops it into the context ([capture](../observing/capture.md)) and lets the person take the next topic; it never guesses ([what is unclear](unclear-goes-to-context.md)). It notices deviations by the person's role document and asks for the reason ([the course of a deviation](../deviations/course.md)). It never builds.
