# The plan follows the model

Type: [Rule](../../core/rule.md)

## Applies to
[The plan](README.md)

## Rule
The plan is cut as the model is: its top level is a module of the model, which lasts and has a role that answers for it. In the plan of a module lie its stories, and its features, which group stories. A [feature](feature.md) is an idea we want to build that is larger than one story, and it ends with its acceptance. A [story](story.md) is complete in itself once its predecessors are done; it has tasks once it is planned, and it needs no feature. Carried out is the story. Work is planned in the module it changes, so everyone has one picture of the project. A story that changes several modules belongs to one of them and names the other places under Realizes. A finding whose module is unclear goes to the model as a whole, where whoever is responsible sorts it. An item's place in the plan is its identity; it has no number.

## Example
"Renew a loan" is a story of the feature "Renew online" in the plan of the module Lending.
