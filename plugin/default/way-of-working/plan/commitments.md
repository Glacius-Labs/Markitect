# A commitment is a story

Type: [Rule](../../core/rule.md)

## Applies to
[The plan](README.md), [the Project manager](../team/project-manager.md), [the Assistant](../team/assistant.md)

## Rule
What a person commits to in a conversation becomes a story that this person does, in the plan of the module it concerns. So does a decision that asks a person to act; it is the commitment of the person the decision names, or else of whoever is responsible for the module it concerns. What the person has done already when the decision is recorded becomes no story: it is noted in the finding's outcome. Otherwise the Project manager writes the story `in progress`, claimed by that person: no agent starts it, and no Builder takes it. Its Plan says what the person committed to; it realizes nothing in the model, so Realizes is `none`; its Tasks hold the one thing the person does; its Origin is the report or finding the decision stands in. The Assistant shows it to the person among what waits for them. When the person has done it, the session they work in sets it `done` and releases the claim. A personal to-do list is no file of its own; it is a view of the plan.

## Example
In the weekly meeting a person promises to ask the members' council about fines. The Project manager writes a story in the plan of the module Lending, claimed by that person; the Assistant shows it to them each morning until they say it is done.
