# The circle

Type: [Activity](../core/activity.md)

## Trigger
Something happens while we develop: someone has an idea, meets resistance or finds a bug; something behaves differently than thought; a meeting decides; a watch sees something in its source.

## Steps
1. **Observe.** What happens lands as a report or a drop in the context. People tell their agent what strikes them or hand it material, and it drops the wording unchanged ([capture](observing/capture.md)). Everyone drops what strikes them ([drop what you notice](observing/drop-what-you-notice.md)) and leaves a report at the end of a session. Each watch looks at its one topic and leaves its report when it is due ([observing](observing/README.md)).
2. **Triage.** The new reports are sifted. Related statements become one finding per topic; each finding gets its type, importance and module and is filed as its type and the three exits demand ([triage](triage/README.md)).
3. **Briefing.** What needs a human stands there briefly, ordered by importance, in the daily and the weekly briefing and the briefing for each meeting. People decide in conversation; the transcript goes into the context ([briefing](briefing/README.md)).
4. **Record.** Decisions are read from the context, noted at their findings and carried out; findings that are `ready` become stories without a decision; what follows from what is decided is added to the model. Afterwards everything stands in the plan or in the model, and each finding is closed with its outcome ([recording](recording/README.md)).
5. **Work.** What stands in the plan is worked: [planning](planning/README.md) with a human, [building](building/README.md) without one, [accepting](accepting/README.md) by the person who accepts. There is only one distinction: does a human have to conceive it first, or can it go through without planning? Once the model says what should hold and the plan stands, an agent carries out the rest on its own. Everything that strikes on the way goes back to step 1.

## Result
What deserves attention is decided and in the plan, what needs no human went to work, and everything stays traceable, with origin and outcome.

## Participants
The [Housekeeper](team/housekeeper.md), which turns steps 1 to 4 at least once a day.
Every person through their agent; the [Briefer](team/briefer.md) and [the Assistant](team/assistant.md), who bring what matters to the people; the people, who decide.

## Notes
Steps 1 to 4 are turned at least once a day, in [the daily run](day/daily-run.md), which may run again at any time.
