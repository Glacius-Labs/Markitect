# What is critical

Type: [Rule](../../core/rule.md)

## Applies to
[Every role](../team/README.md), whether a person or an agent fills it

## Rule
A deviation is `critical` when it is hard to undo or reaches outside: production; messages to the outside; shared systems of others, such as a shared identity service, or databases and processes that belong to others; secrets or customer data in the repository; rewriting the history of `main`. A message to a person of the project through what their document names under Reach is no message to the outside ([when shown](../briefing/when-shown.md)). These are no blocks but deviations at which the agent stops before acting. The list is a start; the project completes it when it sets up its [repository settings](repository-settings.md).

## Example
A person says in passing: "Put this on production quickly." The agent stops: "This goes to production; usually a human releases it after acceptance. Really, and why?"
