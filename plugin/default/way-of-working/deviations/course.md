# The course of a deviation

Type: [Rule](../../core/rule.md)

## Applies to
[Deviations](README.md), [capture](../observing/capture.md), [the Triager](../team/triager.md), [the Project manager](../team/project-manager.md)

## Rule
A deviation takes five steps. Notice: the agent the person works with sees by the role document that the work lies outside the usual, says so in one sentence and asks for the reason; at `low` and `high` it carries on afterwards, at `critical` only after a yes. Net: the watch on [the deviations](../observing/watch-deviations.md) states what happened on `main`, so that what nobody reported shows too. Drop: [capture](../observing/capture.md) leaves a note in the context: who, what, which role would be usual, the level, the reason in the person's words; no new channel. Classify: the Triager files every deviation as a reference to the smell story for this person and this kind of deviation, which the Project manager creates when there is none; its priority is the higher of the level and the pile-up ([priorities](../plan/priorities.md)); from `normal` the Analyst starts, and its report becomes a finding like any other; good reasons piling up make a finding of type Process that adapts the role document. Show: the Briefer and the Assistant show it by importance, as always. A `critical` deviation is filed a second time, as the damage itself: that finding goes to a human at once and stays critical until it is dealt with ([critical until done](../briefing/critical-until-done.md)).
