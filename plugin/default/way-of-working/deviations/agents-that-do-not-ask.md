# Agents that do not ask

Type: [Rule](../../core/rule.md)

## Applies to
Every role that works alone: [Builder](../team/builder.md), [Reviewer](../team/reviewer.md), [Analyst](../team/analyst.md), [Housekeeper](../team/housekeeper.md), [Observer](../team/observer.md), [Triager](../team/triager.md), [Project manager](../team/project-manager.md), [Briefer](../team/briefer.md)

## Rule
Agents that do not ask go through the same course of a deviation. They write the reason down themselves. At `low` and `high` they carry on. At `critical` nobody is there to say yes: they stop and lay a report into the context, as when a build is aborted.
