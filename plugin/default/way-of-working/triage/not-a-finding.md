# What is no finding

Type: [Rule](../../core/rule.md)

## Applies to
[The Triager](../team/triager.md)

## Rule
No finding arises from what the model already says, from a report without anything noteworthy, or from praise, which stays in the context. "Do it later" is no type but a low importance. A commitment goes into the plan as a story. A decision on a finding that exists is no new finding: the Project manager notes it at that finding and carries it out. What was decided where it arose has no finding yet, such as an idea a meeting decides to build, an objection to what was carried out without a decision, or a story a person gives up: it becomes a finding like any other, filed as decided ([the types of findings](finding-types.md)). The finding an objection turns against stays closed; the new one names it in its context.
