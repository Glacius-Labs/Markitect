# Project

Why the project exists, what is given from outside, the guiding principles it follows, and how well its result must work. This part is the top of the spec: the product's capabilities, the architecture and the way of working fit it.

| Part | Holds |
|---|---|
| [Purpose](purpose/README.md) | the problem, the stakeholders and the goals |
| [Context](context/README.md) | the terms of the field and the external systems; constraints and assumptions |
| [Guiding principles](principles/README.md) | the few rules every decision in the project follows |
| [Quality](quality/README.md) | how well the result must work, along the nine characteristics of ISO/IEC 25010 |

Fill it from the top: purpose first, since every other part answers to it. Keep each part short; what the product should do belongs to the product's capabilities, how it is built to the architecture. The library's template `software`, with its six chapters, serves as a checklist for what this part should answer, not as its outline.
