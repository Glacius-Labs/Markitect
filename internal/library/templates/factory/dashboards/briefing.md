# Briefing

Type: [Dashboard](../../../packages/factory/dashboard.md)

## For
everyone, in the daily and the weekly meeting: what is to decide?

## Sections
1. Critical: as in the [Overview](overview.md). Norman says it always.
2. Daily: the findings shown daily ([when shown](../rules/briefing/when-shown.md)), from `.markitect/findings/pending/`. Norman says only the person's own.
3. Weekly: the findings shown weekly, from `.markitect/findings/pending/`. Norman says nothing of it.
4. Without decision: what went into the plan without a decision since the last briefing, from `.markitect/findings/ready/` and `closed/`. Norman says nothing of it.

One line per finding: its title, type and Area, with a link to its file. The view reads the findings itself; of the Briefer's briefing it takes only the date, "today's briefing is there".
