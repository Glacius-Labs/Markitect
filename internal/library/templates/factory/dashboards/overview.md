# Overview

Type: [Dashboard](../../../packages/factory/dashboard.md)

## For
everyone: where do we stand, who does what?

## Sections
1. Critical: the critical findings, one line each, from `.markitect/findings/pending/`. Norman says it always, first.
2. People: for each [person](../people/README.md), their role, their own features, their stories `in progress` with who works on them, Builder or Analyst, and in `acceptance`; from the plan and the people. Norman says only what touches the person.
3. Areas: one line per Area: its features, its stories by state as bars, the size of its housekeeping; from the plan. Norman says nothing of it.
4. Landed: what landed on `main` since the last briefing; from the plan. Norman says it as a number.
5. Briefing: the open findings as a number per importance, leading to the [Briefing](briefing.md); from `.markitect/findings/pending/`. Norman says it as a number.
