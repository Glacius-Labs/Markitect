## Sections

3. Acceptance: the stories in `acceptance`, oldest first, bundled by feature; then the features whose stories are all accepted and that wait as a whole; from the plan. Norman says what waits for me.
4. Around me: what others do that touches me, such as a story that lands soon; from the plan. Norman says it briefly.
5. Without decision: what went into the plan without a decision, open to objection; from `.markitect/findings/ready/` and `closed/`. Norman says it as a number.
6. Proposal: one line from Norman, where to start.
