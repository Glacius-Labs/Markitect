# My work

Type: [Dashboard](../../../packages/factory/dashboard.md)

## For
[Admin](../roles/admin.md): what waits for me, where do I start?

## Sections
1. Critical: as in the [Overview](overview.md). Norman says it always, first.
2. Decisions: the open findings that concern my Areas and features or ask me, by importance; from `.markitect/findings/pending/`. Norman says those with `high`, else the number.
3. My features: for each feature I own, its progress; below it its stories: aborted builds, `ready` (start the build), `draft` (plan it), `in progress` (Builder or Analyst), `acceptance`; from the plan and the abort reports in the context. Norman says what waits for me.
4. Acceptance: the stories in `acceptance`, oldest first, bundled by feature; then the features that wait to be accepted as a whole; from the plan. Norman says what waits for me.
5. My stories: the stories I do myself ([commitments](../rules/plan/commitments.md)); from the plan. Norman says them.
6. Factory watch: the findings of type Factory and Unclassified; whether the Housekeeper ran today; overdue observers; from `.markitect/findings/pending/`, the briefing and the context. Norman says what waits for me.
7. Around me: what others do that touches me: dependencies of my stories, work of others in my features; from the plan. Norman says it briefly.
8. Without decision: what went into the plan in my Areas without a decision, open to objection; from `.markitect/findings/ready/` and `closed/`. Norman says it as a number.
9. Proposal: one line from Norman, where to start.
