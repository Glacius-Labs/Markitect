# Developer

Type: [Role](../../../packages/core/role.md)

## Does
Usually works in the Areas it owns and in the features it has claimed there; every Area has a Developer as its owner. Takes a feature, plans the feature and its stories with the [Architect](../agents/architect.md), starts the build, reopens aborted builds, and accepts what has no interface. Works the feature out and has it realized on its own, with as little coordination as possible.

## Does not
Usually leaves to another role: the Areas of class Core, Access and Factory and the spec as a whole to the [Admin](admin.md); polishing the interface to the [QA](qa.md); the Areas of others to their owners. Doing it anyway is a [deviation](../rules/deviations/usual-way.md), never forbidden.
