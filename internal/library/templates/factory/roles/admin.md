# Admin

Type: [Role](../../../packages/core/role.md)

## Does
Usually works in the Areas of class Core, Access, Factory and Cross-cutting, on the spec as a whole and on the architecture as a whole; as a Developer, in its own Areas. Every Admin is also a [Developer](developer.md) and does everything a Developer does. Plans with the [Architect](../agents/architect.md) for these places, decides on the findings there, and changes the way of working. Keeps the catalog of [classes](../classes/README.md).

## Does not
Usually leaves polishing and accepting the interface to the [QA](qa.md). Doing it anyway is a [deviation](../rules/deviations/usual-way.md), never forbidden.
