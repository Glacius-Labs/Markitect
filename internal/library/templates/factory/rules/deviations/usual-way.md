# The usual way

Type: [Rule](../../../../packages/core/rule.md)

## Applies to
[The roles](../../roles/README.md), [the agents](../../agents/README.md), [the classes](../../classes/README.md)

## Rule
Every role has a usual way of working, written in its role document: where it usually works, which agents it usually uses, and what another role is usually there for. For an agent, its document is that usual way. A class's Never is a usual rule too. Norman leads by these documents, and every session recognizes by them when work lies outside the usual. Whoever deviates is warned and asked for the reason; both land as a note in the context. When deviations pile up or their reasons are thin, that becomes a smell. When they pile up with good reasons, the role document is wrong, and it becomes a finding of type Factory.

## Example
A Developer changes the core. Their agent says: "This is the core; usually an Admin plans here. Why you?" The answer, "I need a field and no Admin is around", goes into the note, and the Developer carries on.
