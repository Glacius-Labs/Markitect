# Rules

What holds in the way of working and could be got wrong if it were not written down. One [rule](../../../packages/core/rule.md) per document, grouped by what it governs. Agents, processes and steps link here instead of repeating a rule.

- [Principles](principles/README.md): the attitude behind everything.
- [The plan](plan/README.md): its tree, the states of a story, claiming, when a story starts, fixed tasks, priorities.
- [Planning](planning/README.md): how a person works out a level of the plan.
- [Building](building/README.md): aborting, the trace from origin to file, tests, production, building cleanly.
- [Triage](triage/README.md): the types of findings and the three exits.
- [Briefing](briefing/README.md): importance and when something is shown.
- [Deviations](deviations/README.md): the usual way of each role, the levels, what is `critical`.
- [The work folder](work/README.md): what lies in `.markitect/`, reports and checkpoints.

To change a rule, raise a finding of type Factory. To add one, write a document of type Rule in the folder it belongs to and link it from that folder's README.
