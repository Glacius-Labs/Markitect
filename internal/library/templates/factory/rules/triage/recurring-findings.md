# A recurring finding goes one level up

Type: [Rule](../../../../packages/core/rule.md)

## Applies to
[The Triager](../../agents/triage.md)

## Rule
The Triager bundles related statements into one finding per topic, so that a long meeting does not flood the briefing. When a finding recurs, it bundles it into a new finding against the concept one level higher, since the problem may lie there and not at the place.

## Example
The same friction with fines returns in three reports. Besides the three, the Triager writes one finding against the Area Lending's concept of fines.
