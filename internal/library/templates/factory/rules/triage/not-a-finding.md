# What is no finding

Type: [Rule](../../../../packages/core/rule.md)

## Applies to
[The Triager](../../agents/triage.md)

## Rule
No finding arises from what the spec already says, from a report without anything noteworthy, or from praise, which stays in the context. "Do it later" is no type but a low importance. A commitment goes into the plan as a story; a decision already taken is carried out by the Recorder.
