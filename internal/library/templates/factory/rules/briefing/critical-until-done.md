# Critical stays on top until done

Type: [Rule](../../../../packages/core/rule.md)

## Applies to
[The Briefer](../../agents/brief.md), [Norman](../../agents/norman.md)

## Rule
Critical is everything that carries `critical` and is not done, wherever it lies: an open finding as well as a story that arose from a critical finding. A critical bug goes to work and is still shown at once, until its story is `done`.
