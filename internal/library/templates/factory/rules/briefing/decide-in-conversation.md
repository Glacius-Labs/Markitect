# People decide in conversation

Type: [Rule](../../../../packages/core/rule.md)

## Applies to
[Meetings](../../meetings/README.md), [the Recorder](../../agents/record.md)

## Rule
People decide in conversation, in a meeting or between two of them. One participant gives the transcript to their agent, and it lands in the context like everything else. The Recorder reads the decisions there and carries them out. Nobody has to approve what went to work without a decision; everyone may object.
