# People decide in conversation

Type: [Rule](../../../../packages/core/rule.md)

## Applies to
[Meetings](../../meetings/README.md), [Norman](../../agents/norman.md), [the Recorder](../../agents/record.md)

## Rule
People decide in conversation. A person alone decides with their agent: they tell Norman, or the session they work in, what they decided on a finding, and the agent drops the decision into the context with [capture](../../agents/capture.md), naming the finding by its link. Where several people decide, in a meeting or between them, one participant gives the transcript to their agent, and it lands in the context the same way. The Recorder reads the decisions there and carries them out. Nobody has to approve what went to work without a decision; anyone may object.
