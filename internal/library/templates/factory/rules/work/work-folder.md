# The work lies in .markitect/

Type: [Rule](../../../../packages/core/rule.md)

## Applies to
[The factory](../../README.md)

## Rule
Everything about the work that is no artifact lies in `.markitect/`, written in the language of the spec and checked like it. `context/` holds what happened: reports and dropped material; it is information only, demands nothing, and stays. Beside it lie the results everyone works with, which belong to no single agent: `findings/` with `pending/`, `ready/` and `closed/`, where the folder is the state; `plan/`, the tree of Areas, features and stories; `briefing/`, with one folder per recurring meeting. The work is not part of the spec: an item is the occasion for a change, not the target, and what lasts of it reaches the spec through the Target changes of its stories ([spec change](../building/spec-change-lands-with-realization.md)).
