# The spec as a whole

Type: [Rule](../../../../packages/core/rule.md)

## Applies to
[Planning](../../processes/planning/README.md), [Admin](../../roles/admin.md)

## Rule
The spec as a whole is usually changed by an Admin; when someone else does it, that is a deviation of level `high`. An addition by the Recorder that only writes down what follows from what is decided is no deviation ([the three exits](../triage/three-exits.md)). After a change to the spec as a whole, the core and the Areas it touches are checked against it.
