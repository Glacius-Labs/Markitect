# The spec as a whole

Type: [Rule](../../../../packages/core/rule.md)

## Applies to
[Planning](../../processes/planning/README.md), [Admin](../../roles/admin.md)

## Rule
The spec as a whole is usually changed by the role whose document says so, by default the Admin; when someone else does it, that is a deviation of level `high`. An addition by the Recorder that only writes down what follows from what is decided is no deviation ([the three exits](../triage/three-exits.md)). A change to the spec lands through a story like every other change; there is no check of its own after it: a human checks the plan and the changed spec, agents build, and what the application shows flows back as a finding.
