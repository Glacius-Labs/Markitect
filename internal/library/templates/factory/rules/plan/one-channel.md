# One channel for work

Type: [Rule](../../../../packages/core/rule.md)

## Applies to
[The plan](README.md)

## Rule
There is one channel for work: the plan. Not even a small thing is fixed on the side; it enters the plan with a fitting priority and runs like everything else. Otherwise nobody could trace what happened and why.

## Example
A Builder notices a wrong label outside its story. It drops it into the context and goes on; the label becomes a bug story.
