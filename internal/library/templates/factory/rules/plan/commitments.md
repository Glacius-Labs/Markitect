# A commitment is a story

Type: [Rule](../../../../packages/core/rule.md)

## Applies to
[The plan](README.md), [the Recorder](../../agents/record.md)

## Rule
What a person commits to in a conversation becomes a story in the feature `housekeeping` of the Area it concerns, whose task names the person who does it. The story stands `in progress`, claimed by that person, so that no agent starts it; the person sets it `done` and releases the claim. A personal to-do list is no file of its own; it is a view of the plan.

## Example
In the weekly meeting a person promises to ask the members' council about fines. The Recorder writes a story in the housekeeping of the Area Lending whose task names the person.
