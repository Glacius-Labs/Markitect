# A commitment is a story

Type: [Rule](../../../../packages/core/rule.md)

## Applies to
[The plan](README.md), [the Recorder](../../agents/record.md), [the Assistant](../../agents/assistant.md)

## Rule
What a person commits to in a conversation becomes a story that this person does, in the feature `housekeeping` of the Area it concerns. So does a decision that asks a person to act; it is the commitment of the person the decision names, or else of the owner of the Area it concerns. The Recorder writes the story `in progress`, claimed by that person: no agent starts it, and no Builder takes it. Its Target picture is what the person committed to, its Plan `the person does it`, its Target changes `none`, its Tasks one task that names the person, its Origin the report or finding the decision stands in. The Assistant shows it to the person among what waits for them. When the person has done it, the session they work in sets it `done` and releases the claim. A personal to-do list is no file of its own; it is a view of the plan.

## Example
In the weekly meeting a person promises to ask the members' council about fines. The Recorder writes a story in the housekeeping of the Area Lending, claimed by that person, whose task names the person; the Assistant shows it to them each morning until they say it is done.
