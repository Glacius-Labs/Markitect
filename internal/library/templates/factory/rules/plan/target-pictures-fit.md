# Every level fits the levels above

Type: [Rule](../../../../packages/core/rule.md)

## Applies to
[The plan](README.md), [planning](../../processes/planning/README.md), [the Reviewer](../../agents/review.md)

## Rule
Every level has its target: the spec as a whole, what the spec says for an Area, the target picture of a feature, the target picture of a story. Each is to be read like an interface or a responsibility, not as a specification, and each fits every level above it, up to the spec as a whole. When a target chafes, the question is whether the spec wobbles or the artifacts are the problem.

## Example
The story "Renew a loan" fits its feature "Renew online", what the spec says for the Area Lending, and the spec's rule on lending periods.
