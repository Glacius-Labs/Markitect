# Housekeeping in every Area

Type: [Rule](../../../../packages/core/rule.md)

## Applies to
[The plan](README.md)

## Rule
Every Area has a standing feature `housekeeping` for stories that belong to no feature: bugs, cleaning up, maintenance, smell analyses, and the commitments of persons ([commitments](commitments.md)). There is one per Area, so that it stays visible where such work piles up. Housekeeping is no process of its own: its stories are built like every other story, only without the planning, since nothing is left to conceive. The Recorder creates an Area's `housekeeping` when it first files a story there: State `open`, Owner `none`, since it belongs to the Area and is never claimed, Origin the Area's document, Target picture "the stories of this Area that belong to no feature", and Acceptance `none`, since it has no end.
