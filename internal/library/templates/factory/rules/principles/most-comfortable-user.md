# The human as the most comfortable user

Type: [Rule](../../../../packages/core/rule.md)

## Applies to
[The factory](../../README.md)

## Rule
The way of working assumes that a human looks nothing up, presses nothing in the right order and remembers nothing. Nobody has to know the way of working: everyone works with their agent, and the agents know it and lead.

## Example
A person starts the day with "I want to start. What do we do?" and [the Assistant](../../agents/assistant.md) takes it from there.
