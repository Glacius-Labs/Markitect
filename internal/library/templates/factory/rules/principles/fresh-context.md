# Fresh context

Type: [Rule](../../../../packages/core/rule.md)

## Applies to
[The factory](../../README.md)

## Rule
Every piece of work gets a fresh session or a fresh subagent, even the smallest. Whoever coordinates starts one session or subagent per task, with exactly one skill; a subagent that does a part of its starter's task, such as a part of an extraction, works under its starter's document.

## Example
Each review round gets a new Reviewer, never the one of the round before.
