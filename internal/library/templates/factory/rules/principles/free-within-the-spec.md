# Free within the spec

Type: [Rule](../../../../packages/core/rule.md)

## Applies to
[The factory](../../README.md)

## Rule
Work that does not change the spec needs no proofs: within the spec is free space. What protects `main` stays: the Reviewer and the targeted tests before a merge. They protect `main`; they are no proof.

## Example
The person who accepts polishes a page within the spec without a story; the result still goes through a Reviewer.
