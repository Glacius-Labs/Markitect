# Free within the spec

Type: [Rule](../../../../packages/core/rule.md)

## Applies to
[The factory](../../README.md)

## Rule
Work that does not change the spec needs no planning: within the spec is free space, as in polishing. What protects `main` stays the same everywhere: the Reviewer, the targeted tests before a merge, and the confirm of each place whose files changed ([one trace](../building/one-trace.md)).

## Example
The person who accepts polishes a page within the spec as part of accepting its story; the result goes through a Reviewer, who confirms the page's place.
