# Handbook of the way of working

Type: [Handbook](../../packages/factory/handbook.md)

## For
Everyone who works on the project, in whichever [role](roles/README.md); by default the [Admin](roles/admin.md).

## Covers
1. [Principles](rules/principles/README.md)
2. [The circle](processes/circle/README.md)
3. [Roles](roles/README.md) and [agents](agents/README.md)
4. [Planning](processes/planning/README.md)
5. [Building](processes/building/README.md)
6. [Accepting](processes/accepting/README.md)
7. [The plan](rules/plan/README.md), [triage](rules/triage/README.md), [briefing](rules/briefing/README.md) and [deviations](rules/deviations/README.md)
8. [Dashboards](dashboards/README.md)

## Form
One page, built from the documents it covers and rebuilt when they change; never edited by hand. One chapter per covered part, in this order, each short enough to read in a few minutes. It tells a person what happens and what they do; the steps of the agents stay in the agents' documents.
