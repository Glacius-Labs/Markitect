# dropped-material

Type: [Observer](../../../packages/factory/observer.md)

## Observes
The material people and agents dropped into the context: transcripts, messages and notes.

## Source
The drops in `.markitect/context/capture/` and the raw material below it in `.raw/`, read as files in the repository; never the reports of other observers, and never the reports agents lay at the end of their sessions, which the Triager reads directly. Observed to: the last drop read.

## Worth a statement
For each drop, the relevant statements, bundled by theme, each with its speaker and its place: file, time mark and speaker. What in a transcript is only noise, or is about something else, stays out. A meeting's transcript becomes a report of the meeting's statements by theme.

## Never in the report
Customer data and secrets; where a statement cannot be shown without them, only the reference stands. The wording of the raw material is never changed or smoothed where it counts.

## Schedule
daily

## Model
Sonnet, effort high
