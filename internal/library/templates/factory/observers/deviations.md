# deviations

Type: [Observer](../../../packages/factory/observer.md)

## Observes
What happened on `main` against the usual way of working of the roles and agents.

## Source
The Git history of `main`, read locally, held against the [role documents](../roles/README.md) and the [agent documents](../agents/README.md): who changed what, whether there was a review, and whether a claim stood. Observed to: the commit up to which it observed.

## Worth a statement
Every change on `main` outside the usual way of the role or agent that made it, with its [level](../rules/deviations/levels.md), also and above all what nobody reported themselves, such as a merge to `main` without a Reviewer.

## Never in the report
Customer data and secrets, also not the secret that was committed: only the reference. No guessed reason: a reason stands only as written in the deviation's note.

## Schedule
daily

## Model
Sonnet, effort high
