# Factory

Type: [Class](../../../packages/factory/class.md)

## Purpose
The way of working itself: its agents and skills, rules and observers, and the factory part of the spec that holds them.

## Connects to
The factory part of the spec, which its skills realize, as code realizes the product.

## Never
Changes the way of working on the side: every change starts as a finding of type Factory.
