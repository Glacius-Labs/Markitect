# Record

Type: [Step](../../../../packages/company/step.md)

## Description
Decisions are read from the context, noted at their findings and carried out; findings in `ready` become stories without a decision; what follows from what is decided is added to the spec. Afterwards everything stands in the plan or in the spec, and each finding lies in `closed` with its outcome; a finding attached to a claimed feature waits in `ready` until the Architect closes it in that feature's planning ([Finding](../../../../packages/factory/finding.md)).

## Performed by
The [Recorder](../../agents/record.md).
