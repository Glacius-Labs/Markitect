# Observe

Type: [Step](../../../../packages/company/step.md)

## Description
What happens lands as a report or a drop in the context. People tell their agent what strikes them or hand it material, and it drops the wording unchanged with [capture](../../agents/capture.md). Every agent drops what strikes it ([drop what you notice](../../rules/work/drop-what-you-notice.md)) and lays its report at the end of its session. Each observer watches its one topic and writes its report on its schedule.

## Performed by
Every person through their agent; every agent with [capture](../../agents/capture.md); the [observers](../../observers/README.md), run by [observe](../../agents/observe.md).
