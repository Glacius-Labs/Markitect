# Briefing

Type: [Step](../../../../packages/company/step.md)

## Description
What needs a human stands there briefly, ordered by importance, in the daily and the weekly briefing and the briefing for each meeting. People decide in conversation ([decide in conversation](../../rules/briefing/decide-in-conversation.md)); the transcript goes into the context.

## Performed by
The [Briefer](../../agents/brief.md) writes; the people decide, in their [meetings](../../meetings/README.md) or between them.
