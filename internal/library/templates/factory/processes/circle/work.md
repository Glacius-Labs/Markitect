## Description
What stands in the plan is worked: [planning](../planning/README.md) with a human, [building](../building/README.md) without one, [accepting](../accepting/README.md) by the person who accepts. There is only one distinction: does a human have to conceive it first, or can it go through without planning? Once target picture and plan stand, an agent carries out the rest on its own. Everything that strikes on the way goes back to Observe.

## Performed by
The [Architect](../../agents/architect.md) with a person; the [Builder](../../agents/build.md), the [Reviewer](../../agents/review.md) and the [Analyst](../../agents/analyze.md); the person who accepts, by default the [Admin](../../roles/admin.md), with [accept](../../agents/accept.md).
