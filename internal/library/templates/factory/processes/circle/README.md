# The circle

Type: [Process](../../../../packages/company/process.md)

## Trigger
Something happens while we develop: someone has an idea, meets resistance or finds a bug; something behaves differently than thought; a meeting decides; an observer sees something in its source.

## Steps
1. [Observe](observe.md): what happens lands as a report in the context.
2. [Triage](triage.md): reports become findings, classified and filed.
3. [Briefing](briefing.md): what needs a human stands there briefly; people decide in conversation.
4. [Record](record.md): decisions are carried out and stand in the plan.
5. [Work](work.md): planning with a human, building without, accepting; what strikes goes back to 1.

## Result
What deserves attention is decided and in the plan, what needs no human went to work, and everything stays traceable as files with origin and outcome.

## Responsible
The [Housekeeper](../../agents/housekeeper.md), which turns stations 1 to 4 once a day.

## Involved
Every person through their agent and [capture](../../agents/capture.md); the [Briefer](../../agents/brief.md) and [Norman](../../agents/norman.md), who bring what matters to the people; the people, who decide.
