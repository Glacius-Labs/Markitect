# Finish

Type: [Step](../../../../packages/company/step.md)

## Description
The planning is closed: the Reviewer checks the plan against the target of its own level; the report on the course of the planning goes into the context; the story is set to `ready` and its claim released; a change to the standing spec goes onto the branch of the story that realizes it ([spec change](../../rules/building/spec-change-lands-with-realization.md)); the plan goes to `main`; the person goes back to Norman, and the chat can be archived.

## Performed by
[finish-planning](../../agents/finish-planning.md), in the planning session; a fresh [Reviewer](../../agents/review.md) checks.
