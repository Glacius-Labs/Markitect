# Planning

Type: [Process](../../../../packages/company/process.md)

## Trigger
A person takes a node of the plan to work out: the spec as a whole, an Area, a feature or a story. [Norman](../../agents/norman.md) starts the [Architect](../../agents/architect.md) for it.

## Steps
1. [Collect context](collect-context.md)
2. [Brief](brief.md)
3. [Collect](collect.md)
4. [Order](order.md)
5. [Summarize](summarize.md)
6. [Check](check.md)
7. [Plan](plan.md)
8. [Go](go.md)
9. [Finish](finish.md)

## Result
The target of the planned level stands right and fits every level above; the plan is on `main`; the story is `ready`, and a change to a standing spec lies on the branch of the story that realizes it. "Planned through" means: the spec stands right.

## Responsible
The person who plans and gives the go: usually the owner of the Area, a [Developer](../../roles/developer.md), and an [Admin](../../roles/admin.md) for the spec as a whole and for the Areas their class has an Admin plan.

## Involved
The [Architect](../../agents/architect.md) leads the conversation and writes; a [Reviewer](../../agents/review.md) checks in steps 6 and 9; [finish-planning](../../agents/finish-planning.md) closes. The rules: [one level at a time](../../rules/planning/one-level-at-a-time.md), [reopen only after a finding](../../rules/planning/reopen-after-finding.md), [what is unclear goes into the context](../../rules/planning/unclear-goes-to-context.md).
