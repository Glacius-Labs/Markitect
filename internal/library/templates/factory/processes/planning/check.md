# Check

Type: [Step](../../../../packages/company/step.md)

## Description
A Reviewer compares what the planning worked out, the target picture of a feature or story, or a change to the spec, as the changed documents on the story's branch, with every level above it, up to the spec as a whole ([every level fits the levels above](../../rules/plan/target-pictures-fit.md)). When one does not fit, work on it continues.

## Performed by
A fresh [Reviewer](../../agents/review.md), started by the [Architect](../../agents/architect.md).
