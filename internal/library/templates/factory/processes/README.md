# Processes

The recurring sequences of the way of working. One folder per process: its README is the [process](../../../packages/company/process.md), and each of its [steps](../../../packages/company/step.md) is a document beside it. The steps say what happens and who does it; the agents' documents say how, and the rules what holds.

- [The circle](circle/README.md): from what happens to findings, decisions and work, and back.
- [Planning](planning/README.md): a person and the Architect work out one level of the plan.
- [Building](building/README.md): a Builder and fresh Reviewers carry out a story.
- [Accepting](accepting/README.md): the person who accepts polishes and accepts what has landed.

The daily run that turns the circle stands in the document of the [Housekeeper](../agents/housekeeper.md).

To add a process, make a folder with the process as its README and one document per step, and link it from this list; to change one, raise a finding of type Factory.
