# Review round

Type: [Step](../../../../packages/company/step.md)

## Description
A fresh Reviewer checks the realization against the plan and the target picture of the story, every task on its own, against every level above, and against the rules of the Area's class. It changes nothing of what was built; what it gives back are `comments`, which the Builder answers. At most five rounds; from the third on, nitpicks and remarks of little value no longer count. Without agreement after them, the Builder [aborts](../../rules/building/abort.md). What remains worth mentioning after the rounds, the Builder drops into the context.

## Performed by
A fresh [Reviewer](../../agents/review.md) per round, started by the [Builder](../../agents/build.md).
