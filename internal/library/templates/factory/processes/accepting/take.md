# Take

Type: [Step](../../../../packages/company/step.md)

## Description
The QA works through the list of stories in `acceptance`, oldest first. The list is shared; whoever takes an item claims it as usual ([claiming](../../rules/plan/claiming.md)). When nothing new is there, the QA looks at whole features.

## Performed by
The [QA](../../roles/qa.md), with [accept](../../agents/accept.md).
