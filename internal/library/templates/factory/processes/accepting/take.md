# Take

Type: [Step](../../../../packages/company/step.md)

## Description
The person who accepts works through the list of stories in `acceptance`, oldest first. The list is shared; whoever takes a story claims it as usual ([claiming](../../rules/plan/claiming.md)). When nothing new is there, they look at whole features; a feature has no field to claim, and its Owner stays.

## Performed by
The person who accepts, by default the [Admin](../../roles/admin.md), with [accept](../../agents/accept.md).
