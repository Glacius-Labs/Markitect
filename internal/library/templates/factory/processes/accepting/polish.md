# Polish

Type: [Step](../../../../packages/company/step.md)

## Description
The QA polishes the interface and its use freely within the spec; details need no story, they are tried and explored. The agent recognizes on its own when a change to the interface would demand a stretch against the concept: it recommends to the QA to discuss the feature anew and writes it into its report. When something fundamental has to change, that is no polishing any more: it is dropped into the context and enters the circle at the front. The QA may also bring in ideas.

## Performed by
The [QA](../../roles/qa.md), with [accept](../../agents/accept.md).
