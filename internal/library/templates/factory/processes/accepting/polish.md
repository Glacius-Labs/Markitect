# Polish

Type: [Step](../../../../packages/company/step.md)

## Description
The person who accepts polishes the interface and its use freely within the spec, as part of accepting the story, on the story's branch ([names in the work](../../rules/work/names.md)); details are tried and explored, not planned. The agent runs `markitect check` and `markitect status` on the story's places and the files it changed before each review round. The agent recognizes on its own when a change to the interface would demand a stretch against the concept: it recommends to discuss the feature anew and writes it into its report. When something fundamental has to change, that is no polishing any more: it is dropped into the context and enters the circle at the front. The person may also bring in ideas.

## Performed by
The person who accepts, by default the [Admin](../../roles/admin.md), with [accept](../../agents/accept.md).
