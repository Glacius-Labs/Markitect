# Resource

Something the business uses: a room, a device, a system, such as the reading room. Take it for each thing whose care someone is responsible for. Take a [role](../core/role.md) instead for who acts, and a [product](product.md) for goods that are sold.

## Description
What it is and how many there are. Example: `The reading room with 12 desks, on the first floor.`

## Responsible
The role that looks after it, as a link. Example: `[Caretaker](../team/caretaker.md)`.
