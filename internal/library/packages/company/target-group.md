# Target group

A group of customers the business addresses, such as school classes. Take it for each group whose wishes differ from the others'. Take a [role](../core/role.md) instead for who works in the business.

## Description
Who they are and what matters to them. Example: `Teachers who visit with a class; they need quiet time slots and several copies of one book.`

## Buys
The services and products they buy, as links. Example: `[Class visit](../offer/class-visit.md)`.
