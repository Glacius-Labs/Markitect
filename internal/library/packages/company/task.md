# Task

Recurring work that belongs to one role, such as counting the cash box. Take it for work that returns on a schedule or an occasion and has no sequence of steps. Take a [process](process.md) instead for a sequence with a trigger and a result.

## Description
What is done. Example: `Count the cash box and note the sum in the day book.`

## Responsible
The role that does it, as a link. Example: `[Librarian](../team/librarian.md)`.

## When
How often, or on what occasion. Example: `Every evening at closing time.`

## Checked by (optional)
The role that checks it, as a link. Example: `[Head librarian](../team/head-librarian.md)`.
