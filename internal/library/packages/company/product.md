# Product

Goods the business sells, such as a tote bag. Take it for each good with a price. Take a [service](service.md) instead for work done for a customer.

## Description
What it is. Example: `A cloth bag printed with the library logo.`

## Price
The price or price range, with currency. Example: `6 EUR`.

## Sold by
The roles that sell it, as links. Example: `[Librarian](../team/librarian.md)`.
