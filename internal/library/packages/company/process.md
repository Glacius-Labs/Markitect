# Process

A recurring sequence of work with a trigger and a result, such as lending a book. Take it for each sequence that is repeated and has someone accountable. Take a [task](task.md) instead for recurring work that belongs to one role and has no sequence, and a [step](step.md) for one stage of a process.

## Trigger
What starts it. Example: `A member asks for a book at the desk.`

## Steps
The steps in order, as links or as a numbered list. Example: `1. Find the copy. 2. Record the loan. 3. Hand over the copy.`

## Result
What holds when it ends. Example: `The copy is on loan to the member, with a due date.`

## Responsible
The role or agent accountable for it, as a link. Example: `[Librarian](../team/librarian.md)`.

## Involved (optional)
Further roles or agents and how they take part, as links. Example: `[Volunteer](../team/volunteer.md) shelves the returned copies.`
