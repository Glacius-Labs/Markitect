# Step

One step of a process, such as recording a loan. Take it when a step has someone to perform it or its own detail; a short step can stay a line in the process's list. Take a [task](task.md) instead for recurring work outside a process.

## Description
What is done. Example: `Scan the member's card and the copy, and record the loan with its due date.`

## Performed by
The roles or agents that do it, as links. Example: `[Librarian](../../team/librarian.md)`.

## Under supervision (optional)
The roles or agents that may do it only under supervision, as links. Example: `[Trainee](../../team/trainee.md)`.
