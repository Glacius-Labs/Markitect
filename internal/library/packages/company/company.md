# Company

The description of a business as a whole. Its sections are the table of contents: each links to the documents of its part. Take it once per business, as the README at its top. Take a [process](process.md) or a [service](service.md) for one thing inside it.

## Purpose
What the business is for: mission, business model and [goals](../core/goal.md), as links. Example: `[A lending library for the town](purpose/README.md)`.

## Offer
The [services](service.md) and [products](product.md), as links. Example: `[Lending a book](offer/lending.md)`.

## Customers
The [target groups](target-group.md), as links. Example: `[Town residents](customers/residents.md)`.

## Team
The [roles](../core/role.md), as links. Example: `[Librarian](team/librarian.md)`.

## Processes
The [processes](process.md), as links. Example: `[Lend a book](processes/lend-a-book.md)`.

## Rules
The [rules](../core/rule.md) that hold across the business, as links. Example: `[At most five open loans](rules/five-open-loans.md)`.

## Parts (optional)
Further parts with a template of their own, each as a link to its README. Example: `[Children's section](children/README.md)`.
