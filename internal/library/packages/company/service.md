# Service

Work the business does for a customer and charges for, such as a book search. Take it for each such work with a price. Take a [product](product.md) instead for goods, and a [process](process.md) for how the work is done inside.

## Description
What the service includes and what it leaves out. Example: `The librarian searches other libraries for a title. Delivery of the book is not included.`

## Price
The price, with currency. Example: `3 EUR per search`.

## Performed by
The roles that may do it on their own, as links. A role not named here or under supervision may not do it. Example: `[Librarian](../team/librarian.md)`.

## Under supervision (optional)
The roles that may do it only while a qualified person watches, as links. Example: `[Trainee](../team/trainee.md)`.

## Duration (optional)
How long it takes. Example: `About 15 minutes.`
