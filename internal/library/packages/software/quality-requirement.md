# Quality requirement

How well the system must do one thing, with a measure that decides whether it does. Take it for each requirement on speed, reliability, security or another quality that someone could check. Take a [rule](../core/rule.md) instead for what must hold without degree, and an [operation](operation.md) for what the system does.

## Requirement
What must hold. Example: `The member page shows the open loans quickly, also on a weak connection.`

## Measure
The number and unit that decide whether it holds. Example: `95 percent of page views finish within 2 seconds on a 3 Mbit/s line.`

## Matters to (optional)
The stakeholders it matters to, as links. Example: `[Members](../purpose/members.md)`.
