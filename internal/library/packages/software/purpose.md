# Purpose

Why the system exists: the problem it removes, for whom, and what it must achieve. Take it once per system, as the README of its purpose part. Take a [goal](../core/goal.md) for a single aim, and the [domain](domain.md) for what the system does.

## Problem
The problem it removes, in a few sentences. Example: `Loans are kept on paper cards; members cannot see what they owe, and the desk loses time searching.`

## Stakeholders
The [stakeholders](stakeholder.md), as links, one per line. Example: `[Members](members.md)`.

## Goals
What it must achieve, as links to [goals](../core/goal.md). Example: `[Members renew a loan without visiting the desk](renew-without-desk.md)`.

## Not included (optional)
What it deliberately leaves out. Example: `Buying books; the library does that by hand.`
