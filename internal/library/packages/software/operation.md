# Operation

One thing a caller can do to the system or ask of it, such as lending a copy. Take it for each action or question whose result a caller relies on. Take a [concept](../core/concept.md) instead for a thing the system manages, and an [interface](interface.md) for the way in.

## Does
What it achieves. Example: `Gives a member a copy for one lending period and records the loan.`

## Who
The roles that may call it, as links. Example: `[Librarian](../team/librarian.md)`.

## Given
What the caller supplies. Example: `The member's card number and the copy's number.`

## Refused when (optional)
Each condition under which it is refused, with its reason and the answer the caller gets. Example: `The member has five open loans: refused, answer "limit reached".`

## Returns (optional)
What it gives back. Example: `The loan with its due date.`
