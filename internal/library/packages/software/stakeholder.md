# Stakeholder

A group of people who use the system or are affected by it, named by what they do, such as members or librarians. Take it for each such group. Take a [role](../core/role.md) instead for who acts inside the system or business, and a [goal](../core/goal.md) for what a group wants reached.

## Who
Who they are, in one sentence. Example: `People with a library card who borrow books.`

## Wants
What they want from the system. Example: `To see their loans and renew them without a visit.`

## Today (optional)
How they work today, without the system. Example: `They ask at the desk, where the librarian looks up a paper card.`
