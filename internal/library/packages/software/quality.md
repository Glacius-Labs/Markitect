# Quality

How well the system must work, along the nine characteristics of ISO/IEC 25010:2023. Each characteristic says what the system requires of it, with links to quality requirements, or why it requires nothing.

## Requirements
The [quality requirements](quality-requirement.md), as links.

## Functional suitability (optional)
How completely, correctly and appropriately the functions serve the stated needs.

## Performance efficiency (optional)
Time, resources and capacity under the stated conditions.

## Compatibility (optional)
Working beside other products and exchanging information with them.

## Interaction capability (optional)
How well the intended users can learn, operate and use it.

## Reliability (optional)
Doing what it must, without failure, for the time it must, and recovering when it fails.

## Security (optional)
Protecting information and data, so that people and systems have only the access they are allowed.

## Maintainability (optional)
How effectively and efficiently it can be changed.

## Flexibility (optional)
Adapting to changed requirements, contexts of use and environments.

## Safety (optional)
Avoiding states in which human life, health, property or the environment is endangered.
