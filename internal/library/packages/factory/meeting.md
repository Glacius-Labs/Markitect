# Meeting

A recurring meeting of the team, in which people decide in conversation. The Briefer writes a briefing for each one in time, and the Assistant says on the morning of its day that it is due and that its briefing lies ready. Take it for every recurring meeting. A meeting decides; it writes no plan: one participant gives the transcript to their agent, it lands in the context, and the Recorder carries out what was decided.

## Rhythm
How often it takes place: `daily`, `weekly`, or the days. Example: `weekly, on Mondays`.

## Time
When it starts, in the project's time zone. Example: `09:30`.

## Participants
The persons or roles who take part, as links. Example: `every [person](../people/README.md)`.

## Covers
What is discussed there, and which briefing it takes. Example: `The weekly briefing: findings of importance normal and low.`
