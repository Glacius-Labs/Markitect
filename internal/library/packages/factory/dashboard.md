# Dashboard

A view for people, described in the spec: for whom it is and what it shows in which order. The page itself is an artifact built from this document and from the work in `.markitect/` as it lies on `main`. A dashboard stores nothing: it shows plan, findings, context and briefings, and links to their files. Take it for each view a role wants to see. Do not take it to describe the way of working, which stands in its own documents.

## For
The roles or persons it is for, as links, or `everyone`; then the one question it answers. Example: `[Admin](../roles/admin.md): what waits for me, where do I start?`

## Sections
The blocks in their order, one per line: the name, what it shows, where it reads it, and what the Assistant says of it in the morning. Example: `1. Critical: the critical findings, one line each, from .markitect/findings/pending/. The Assistant says it always, first.`
