# Handbook

A handbook for people, described in the spec: for whom it is, what it covers and what it looks like. The handbook itself is an artifact built from the parts of the spec it covers; its content stands once, in the spec, and the handbook is rebuilt when they change. Take it for each handbook the project builds, such as the handbook of the way of working from the factory part, or a user handbook from the product part. Do not write the handbook's content here.

## For
Who reads it, as links to roles or in a sentence. Example: `everyone who works on the project`.

## Covers
The parts and documents of the spec it is built from, as links, in the order the reader meets them. Example: `[Principles](rules/principles/README.md)`, then `[The circle](processes/circle/README.md)`.

## Form
What it looks like: one page or several, its chapters and how long they may be, and where it is published. Example: `One page, one chapter per covered part, each readable in two minutes.`
