# Person

A human who works on the project, written down once so that Areas, features, meetings and dashboards can link to them. Take it for every human who holds a role. A [role](../core/role.md) says what someone usually does; a person says who holds it. An agent is no person: take an [agent](agent.md).

## Roles
The roles this person holds, as links, one per line. A person may hold several. Example: `[Admin](../roles/admin.md)`.
