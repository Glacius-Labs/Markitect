# Decision

A choice that was made, with its reason, so that it is not reopened without new evidence. Take it for each choice that someone could undo by mistake, such as a technology or a way of working. Take a [rule](rule.md) instead for something that holds without a choice behind it, and a [goal](goal.md) for what should be reached.

## Decision
What was chosen, in a sentence. Example: `Loans are kept per copy, not per title.`

## Why
The reason, and what speaks against it. Example: `The desk needs to know which copy is out; against it, a title with many copies needs a count.`

## Alternatives (optional)
What was weighed and not taken, one per line. Example: `Loans per title with a counter of free copies.`

## Reopen when (optional)
The evidence that would reopen the decision. Example: `When the library lends digital copies that have no shelf.`
