# Role

Someone who acts and is named by what they usually do: a person, a group, an office or a system, such as the librarian. Take it for each kind of actor whom the spec lets do or decide something. In this product a role forbids nothing: what someone does outside the usual way of their role is a deviation, which is noticed, not blocked. Take a person's own document instead for who holds a role, where the spec has such documents.

## Does
What the role does, with links to what it acts on. Example: `Lends and takes back copies at the desk; keeps the [loans](loan.md) up to date.`

## Does not (optional)
What the role usually leaves to another role, with a link to it. Example: `Orders new books; the [acquisitions clerk](acquisitions-clerk.md) does that.`

## Reports to (optional)
The role it answers to, as a link. Example: `[Head librarian](head-librarian.md)`.
