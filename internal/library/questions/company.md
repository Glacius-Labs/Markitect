# Questions: company

Questions across documents that a company spec should answer. Each answer comes from the spec alone, with a citation.

- Who may do each service alone, and who only under supervision?
- What does each service and product cost, and who may sell it?
- Who is responsible for each process, and what starts it?
- Which role does each recurring task, how often, and who checks it?
- Which rules hold across the business, and whom do they bind?
- Which target group buys which services and products?
- Which resources does each process use, and who looks after them?
