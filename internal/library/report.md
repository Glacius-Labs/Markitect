# Report

A signal that a change is needed: an idea of the owner, a bug report, an incident, a wish, a log, a finding of a job or a problem with the spec itself. It says what is wrong or wanted, with evidence, and proposes no solution. A job writes the first five sections; triage adds the rating, the actions and the closing, and the job that does the action adds the prevention.

## Source
Who or what raised it: a job and its role, intake, or the owner. Only a conversation sets the owner.

## Place
The document, artifact or path it concerns, as a path.

## Finding
What is wrong or wanted, with the evidence.

## Occurred or risk
Whether it happened, as an error, or may happen, as a risk.

## Violated property
One of the five properties of a good spec, necessary, complete, consistent, unambiguous and verifiable, or structured, or correct when the spec says the wrong thing.

## Rating (optional)
Impact and likelihood, set by triage.

## Actions (optional)
The branch that answers it, or the proposals for the owner, set by triage.

## Prevention (optional)
The fixed question or counter-check the action adds, so that the same gap shows earlier next time.

## Closing (optional)
How it was closed, and why.
