# Alternative

An Alternative Definition states one alternative that was considered and not chosen, and why. In the folder of a [[std/Decision]], under `alternatives/`, it is an alternative the Decision considered. Under `purpose/alternatives/` it is another way to remove the project's [[std/Problem|Problems]] than building the system, such as keeping the paper book or renting a service. It does not bind.

Verified by nothing; it is read, not checked.
