# Stakeholder

A Stakeholder Definition says in one sentence who a group of people is, by what they do and not by who they are, that uses the system or is affected by it. It lives under `purpose/stakeholders/`.

Its [[std/Practice|Practices]], [[std/Concern|Concerns]], [[std/Wish|Wishes]], [[std/Benefit|Benefits]] and [[std/Drawback|Drawbacks]] live in the folder of its name, under `today/`, `concerns/`, `wishes/`, `benefits/` and `drawbacks/`. So one Stakeholder folder holds their whole case: what they do today, what matters to them, what they want, what they gain and what they give. A group that is affected and does not use the system has no Wishes.

Verified when its parts are, and for a group without parts when nothing in the project contradicts what its sentence says.
