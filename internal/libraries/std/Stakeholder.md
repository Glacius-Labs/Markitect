# Stakeholder

A Stakeholder Definition says in one sentence who a group of people is, by what they do and not by who they are, that uses the system or is affected by it. It lives under `purpose/stakeholders/`.

Its [[std/Concern|Concerns]] and [[std/Wish|Wishes]] live in the folder of its name, under `concerns/` and `wishes/`. A group that is affected and does not use the system has no Wishes.

Verified when its parts are, and for a group without parts when nothing in the project contradicts what its sentence says.
