# Wish

A Wish Definition states one thing a [[std/Stakeholder]] wants to do with the system, in their own words. An [[std/Operation]], a [[std/Reaction]] or a [[std/Task]] that exists for it names it in its **Satisfies** field.

Verified when an Operation, a Reaction or a Task that names it exists.
