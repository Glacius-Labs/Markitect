# Term

A Term Definition says what a thing of the world is, where the system does not manage it. It lives under `context/terms/`: what it states is given and holds whatever the system does.

It has, in this order:

- The definition, in one or two sentences, in the words the people of the domain use.
- **Not:** what it is easily confused with, where a neighbour is close. Leave the field out only when nothing is close.

A Term links other Terms only. A thing the system keeps and changes is a [[std/Concept]].

Verified when nothing in the project uses the word in another meaning.
