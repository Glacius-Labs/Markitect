# Benefit

A Benefit Definition states what one [[std/Stakeholder]] gains with the system, compared with today. It is a part of the Stakeholder, under `benefits/` in the folder of its name.

**Instead of:** the [[std/Practice]] or [[std/Problem]] of today that the gain replaces. **Serves:** the [[std/Concern|Concerns]] of the Stakeholder it serves.

Verified when the behavior its Stakeholder uses brings it about and nothing in the project works against it.
