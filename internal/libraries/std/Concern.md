# Concern

A Concern Definition states one thing that matters to a [[std/Stakeholder]]. **Answered by:** the [[std/Goal]], [[std/Constraint]] or [[std/Quality]] that answers it, where one does.

Verified when what **Answered by** names exists, and nothing in the project works against the concern.
